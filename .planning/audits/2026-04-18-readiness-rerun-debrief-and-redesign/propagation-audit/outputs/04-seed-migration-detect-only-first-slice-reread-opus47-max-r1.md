## What The Landed Detect-Only Slice Now Makes More Explicit

The earlier uplift-side posture route carried four distinct but compressed pieces of seed-family memory: a classified posture keyword, per-version counts, small example lists, and the held-later register's undifferentiated "legacy seed corpus migration — held" line. Seeds entered the operator's field of view as aggregates. The landed slice widens the field along seven dimensions.

1. **Per-seed identity instead of aggregate counts.** `build_seed_entry` now produces a structured record carrying `seed_id` (preferring frontmatter `id`), `title`, `rel_path`, `contract_vintage` (`legacy_unversioned`, `current_contract`, or `noncurrent:<version>`), `missing_frontmatter_keys`, `missing_section_headings`, `migration_moves`, and `route_state` (per entry: `migration_candidate` or `current_contract_visible`). The uplift-side posture route only expanded as far as the first five example paths for each legacy or noncurrent bucket. The earlier posture route only surfaced "these files are legacy / noncurrent." The new list carries candidate remediation intent without performing any rewrite — the distinction the slice works hardest to preserve.

3. **A distinct durable-artifact pair named away from uplift memory.** `.planning/SEED-MIGRATION-REPORT.md` and `.planning/SEED-MIGRATION-MANIFEST.json` are new top-level planning artifacts named independently of `UPLIFT-REPORT.md` / `UPLIFT-MANIFEST.json`. ## Where The Live Chain Still Compresses Distinct Jobs

Seven places where helper, workflow, wrapper, uplift handoff, durable outputs, or propagation refresh still flatten distinct work into one surface.

1. **Helper `REQUIRED_FRONTMATTER_KEYS` and `REQUIRED_SECTION_HEADINGS` are a frozen mirror of plant-seed.** The helper's idea of "current-contract shape" is a seven-key tuple and six-heading tuple hard-coded at the top of `seed_migration_inventory.py`. The live plant-seed producer contract lives in the overlay workflow and its consumer-follow-through tests. Nothing ties the helper's lists back to the producer. If `plant-seed.md` adds a new required section (a plausible move under the ongoing seed-family widening family) the helper will keep reporting the new-shape seeds as current-contract-visible with no missing sections, because the helper never learned about the new section. The `contract_vintage` field stays correct; the shape-gap field goes silently wrong. This is the most important producer-follow-through compression in the slice: the detect-only guarantee weakens with producer drift, and no test catches that drift.

2. **`route_state = "dormant"` collapses two meanings.** `analyze_repo` computes `route_state = "surfaced" if migration_candidates else "dormant"`. "Dormant" therefore covers both "corpus exists, every seed is current-shape" and "no corpus at all." The `reasons` list and the `seed_corpus_posture.posture` field do distinguish these cases, but any later consumer that reads only `route_state` loses the distinction. The live repo run returns `route_state: "dormant"` with `posture: "no_seed_corpus"`, and that combined signal can only be decomposed by reading two fields rather than one.

3. **The detect-only uplift route only branches on vintage, not on shape.** `seed_corpus_needs_attention()` returns true only when `legacy_unversioned_count > 0` or `noncurrent_version_total > 0`. The uplift workflow's handoff text mirrors that: it routes operators at `$gsd-seed-migration-inventory --write` when vintage is mixed, but a repo whose seeds all stamp `seed_contract_version: 2` yet miss a required section will never hear about the specialist packet from uplift. The detect-only helper catches shape gaps (test 1, SEED-002 carries `scope` missing and `Strengthening Carry` missing), but the uplift route treats those gaps as invisible. Shape drift in an otherwise current-version corpus therefore has no discovery path from re-entry into the specialist packet.

4. **The written report keeps pre-write recommendation text.** `render_report(analysis)` renders the recommendation string from `analysis["recommendation"]`, which for a surfaced route says "Write the seed-migration inventory when you want durable migration-planning memory." `write_outputs` writes that rendered report even when `--write` has just fired. The neighboring `project_uplift.py` carries a `post_write_analysis` that updates `recommendation_reasons`, `recommend_detect_only`, and `pending_doctrine_sensitive_proposals` before render time, so the durable `UPLIFT-REPORT.md` does not tell the operator to run `--write` in the same file that was just written. The seed-migration helper skips that step. A reader opening a committed `SEED-MIGRATION-REPORT.md` two months after the write sees a recommendation pointing at an action that has already happened.

5. **The uplift SKILL wrapper bundles "deeper packet" with "durable write."** `gsd-uplift-project/SKILL.md` reads "If the operator wants the deeper detect-only migration packet rather than only uplift-side posture, point them at `$gsd-seed-migration-inventory --write`." The flag `--write` is a durable side effect (creates two files under `.planning/`). Wanting "deeper detail" and wanting "durable memory" are different operator choices. The wrapper compresses them. An operator who wants to see the inventory without writing gets routed to a command that writes by default.

6. **The workflow's supporting-reading packet reads the whole corpus.** `<supporting_reading>` says `.planning/seeds/SEED-*.md` "only when a seed corpus exists." Once the helper has inventoried the corpus and emitted migration candidates, only the candidate files are load-bearing for the operator's next read. The supporting-reading text invites rereading every seed rather than the flagged subset, which is larger than the detect-only route actually needs.

7. **Durable output contract not mirrored into the evidence index.** The evidence-index and semantic-map entries for `seed_migration_inventory_workflow` and `seed_migration_inventory_helper` point to the implementation note and the two test files, but no evidence entry captures a concrete durable-output sample (e.g., a committed `SEED-MIGRATION-REPORT.md` from a fixture corpus). Compare with `uplift_manifest_current` (evidence entry type `producer_scoped_report` pointing at the live `.planning/UPLIFT-MANIFEST.json`). The seed-migration durable artifact has no equivalent anchor — its contract shape is only visible through source reading (`render_report`, `write_outputs`) rather than through a frozen artifact that a later reader can diff against.

Taken together, the no-corpus posture leaves three parts of the slice meaningfully exercised (helper internals, overlay ownership, propagation-registry typing) and five parts only exercised at the contract-text level (workflow wording, wrapper wording, uplift handoff firing, durable output shape, fingerprint drift). The reread should treat the first three as land-anchored and the last five as shape-anchored-but-unexercised, with the next move picked to exercise at least one of the latter rather than to open a new unexercised family.

The next-family question the packet names — later rewrite/normalization, broader audit-open, wider entry-wrapper retrofit — stays live behind this narrower move. The point is to exercise one unexercised consumer edge on real operator re-entry before any of those larger families opens.
