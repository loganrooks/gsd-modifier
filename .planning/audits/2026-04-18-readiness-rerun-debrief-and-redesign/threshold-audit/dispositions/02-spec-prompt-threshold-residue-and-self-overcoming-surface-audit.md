## Where Threshold Logic Is Structurally Appropriate

- [d:c+i] Verification surfaces are legitimate homes for threshold logic when they are recording concrete behavioral outcomes or enforcing explicit acceptance contracts. `verify-phase` explicitly records command results as `pass/fail (or SKIPPED if no fixtures)` and routes failure into verification gaps, which is a real gate rather than a disguised planning philosophy. Source: [.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/verify-phase.md](/home/rookslog/workspace/projects/prix-guesser/.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/verify-phase.md:251).
- [d:c+i] `spec-phase` is also a legitimate gate surface when it is locking falsifiable requirements and explicit acceptance criteria. Its use of pass/fail checkboxes and ambiguity gates belongs to that narrow contract-setting job. Sources: [.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/spec-phase.md](/home/rookslog/workspace/projects/prix-guesser/.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/spec-phase.md:128), [.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/spec-phase.md](/home/rookslog/workspace/projects/prix-guesser/.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/spec-phase.md:211), [.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/spec-phase.md](/home/rookslog/workspace/projects/prix-guesser/.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/spec-phase.md:246).
- [d:c+i] Plan-checking is partly gate-shaped too, but it already carries stronger doctrine than a thin pass/fail test because it checks future-awareness, preserved seams, lone high-signal criticism, and later-audit pressure rather than only checking formal completeness. Source: [.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/plan-phase.md](/home/rookslog/workspace/projects/prix-guesser/.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/plan-phase.md:849).
- [d:r:i] So the question is not whether threshold logic should disappear everywhere. It should stay where a surface is genuinely deciding admission, proof, or falsifiability. It becomes contaminating when it starts shaping terrain disclosure, research framing, or planning inquiry.

- [d:c+i] `discuss-phase` still carries a local threshold residue when it decides whether an area is `sufficiently clear` before moving on. That is useful pacing logic, but as phrased it asks for closure-readiness rather than asking what further pressure or optionality might still be worth surfacing before moving on. That keeps evaluation from outrunning the terrain, but it also risks training users and agents to ask for a threshold crossing instead of asking what richer field-mapping or opportunity-disclosure still remains. ## What Is Still Thin Or Diffuse

- [d:r:i] There is still no single workflow, reference, or skill whose primary job is to ask: what stronger forms, optionalities, self-transformations, or opportunity surfaces are available beyond merely satisfying the current gate?
- [d:r:i] The strongest existing positive logic is distributed across review, future-preservation, and seed capture. That means it appears after planning pressure has already narrowed, or only when a user explicitly plants a future idea, rather than being a first-class planning companion.
- [d:r:i] Research also lacks an explicit positive mode. `terrain mapping` avoids premature ranking, which is good, but there is no named `opportunity amplification`, `strength mapping`, or `self-overcoming` mode that asks what the field enables, what stronger forms are visible, and what transformation surfaces deserve deliberate cultivation.
- [d:r:i] Planning lacks a paired companion to verification. Verification can rightly ask whether a contract or behavior held. The harness still needs a distinct companion surface that asks what stronger carry, optionality, or horizon-preservation could be won even after correctness is established.
