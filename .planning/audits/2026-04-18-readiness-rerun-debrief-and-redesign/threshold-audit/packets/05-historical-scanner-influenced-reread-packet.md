# Historical Scanner-Influenced Reread Packet

## Purpose

- [g:r:i] This packet scopes a bounded historical reread over the commits and surfaces most likely to have been shaped by scanner-led cleanup pressure.
- [g:r:i] The task is not a word-counting exercise. The task is to reread historical edits in context and decide where scanner influence widened attention usefully, where it distorted doctrine or prompt/governance carry, and which older artifacts now deserve revisit or patch.

## Governing Questions

1. [d:r:i] Which scanner-influenced edits or follow-on artifacts preserved explicit anti-threshold teaching clearly?
2. [d:r:i] Which edits weakened explicit anti-pattern naming, introduced scanner-as-gate carry, or otherwise laundered doctrine into euphemism or binary-gate cleanup?
3. [d:r:i] Which historical artifacts now deserve:
   - `keep`
   - `contextual reread only`
   - `patch`
   - `revisit the earlier judgment`
4. [d:r:i] Are there recurring patterns in how scanner pressure moved from widening aid into wording control or governance drift?
