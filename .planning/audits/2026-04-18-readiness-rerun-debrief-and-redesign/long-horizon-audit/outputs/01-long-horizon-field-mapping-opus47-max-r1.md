## Cross-Horizon Tensions

- [d:r:i] The register rightly notes that tensions are currently `managed by good planning craft and by the existence of future_awareness, not by a dedicated tension-handling mechanism`. Primary-source confirmation: `<working_model>` and `<epistemic_guardrails>` in CONTEXT carry *uncertainty* across time, but neither structure names *tension between horizons*. A working assumption is not the same thing as `we are deliberately spending near-term optionality to keep long-term optionality`. Source: [.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/discuss-phase.md](/home/rookslog/workspace/projects/prix-guesser/.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/discuss-phase.md:994), [.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/discuss-phase.md](/home/rookslog/workspace/projects/prix-guesser/.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/discuss-phase.md:1023).
- [d:r:i] Explicit tensions currently carried only by operator judgment:
  - **near-term success vs seam preservation**: verify-phase checks goal achievement, but a phase that achieves its goal by quietly binding a preserved seam passes without flag.
  - **milestone goal selection vs long-arc doctrine**: `new-milestone.md` can incorporate seeds and goals without checking them against `LONG-ARC.md`'s protected bets.
  - **anti-threshold quality bar vs ship-adjacent vocabulary**: the root quality-bar doctrine bans `passes`, `ready`, `good enough` language, but verify-phase itself is a gate and emits `passed` / `gaps_found` / `human_needed` status. The tension is legitimate (literal gates are allowed); what is missing is a structural distinction between `gate-language in a gate` and `threshold-language in doctrine prose`.
  - **execution-capacity model vs enacted-budget reality**: proposal 28 names this tension but no workflow has a consumer for it. Arguments against bounded interventions on `size`, `risk`, or `too much` grounds are not required to cite the execution model they assume.
  - **seam activation vs silent premature binding**: proposal 27 names this tension but has no lifecycle slot; seams can silently bind by implementation during a phase that never claimed to decide them.
- [d:r:i] The register's `Horizon-tension register` is the right family but the wrong shape. A register alone is data; what is missing is *structural consumers* — hooks in verify-phase, transition, new-milestone, and complete-milestone that read horizon tensions the way plan-checker already reads future-awareness.
- [d:r:i] There is also a second-order tension the register does not name: **auto-advance pipelines vs deliberation**. `--auto` and `--chain` collapse discuss → plan → execute → verify into a single pass. Auto-advance in exploratory mode is already explicitly bounded (hard stop on missing CONTEXT unless `--allow-no-context`), but there is no equivalent gate for "auto-advance is touching preserve-only seams." ### Propagation Pattern

- [d:r:i] A change at CONTEXT propagates naturally through the strong-carry pipe: add a bucket, four downstream surfaces consume it. - [d:r:i] The milestone-boundary gaps propagate in a different direction: changing `complete-milestone.md` does not change `new-milestone.md`, and vice versa. ## What This Changes About The Harness Improvement Program

### Map First

- [d:r:i] The harness has an asymmetric carry topology. Long-horizon thinking is strongest at phase entry (discuss → context → plan → plan-checker), weakens at plan-execution boundary, goes silent at verify → transition → milestone-close → retrospective, and has no dedicated cross-phase artifact between LONG-ARC.md (doctrine) and STATE.md (digest).
- [d:r:i] The deeper structural pattern: the harness carries *decisions* well (CONTEXT locks, PLAN executes, VERIFY checks) and carries *uncertainty* reasonably (working model, epistemic guardrails, ambiguity scores). It carries *non-decisions* weakly — explicit non-decisions live in CONTEXT, are inherited by plan, and then disappear.
- [d:r:i] The harness has two partially-built systems that are not yet wired to each other:
  - a future-aware planning system (CONTEXT → plan-checker)
  - a debt-aware completion system (`completion_mode`, `audit-uat`, `progress.md` routes)
  Wiring them — specifically, making preservation-debt a first-class completion condition — is a propagation-dense move. It changes transition, progress, new-milestone, and complete-milestone simultaneously.
- [d:r:i] Typing infrastructure exists (claim types, Future-Flexibility Statusing, completion-mode vocabulary) and is richer than the register credits. The problem is consumer absence, not typing absence.
- [d:r:i] The program should not collapse into `fix verify-phase first`. Verify-phase is the most-named downstream gap but the highest-propagation-yield *lever* is upstream: roadmap-time preservation criteria, because their absence is what lets verify-phase fall back to success-only must-haves.
- [d:r:i] The anti-threshold doctrine is load-bearing and cross-cutting. Every intervention proposed here must be evaluated against the risk that it hardens into a checklist that agents satisfy in form rather than honor in substance.

### Bounded Follow-Through Candidates

- [d:r:i] Candidates ordered by propagation yield and upstream-origin pressure, not by ease. These are not a roadmap; they are the narrow set of moves the mapping already implies.
  1. **Extend completion_mode with preservation-debt.** Reuses existing debt-aware machinery; propagates through transition, progress, and complete-milestone with minimal new structure.
  2. **Add preservation criteria to `gsd-roadmapper`.** Upstream origin of the verify-phase gap; propagates downward into verify-phase must-haves and plan-checker fail conditions.
  3. **Add a seam register (either in `LONG-ARC.md` or as a sibling artifact family)** that consumes proposal 27's activation-trigger doctrine. Gives three currently orphaned workflows (new-milestone validation, verify-phase preservation check, complete-milestone preservation audit) a typed input.
  4. **Extend SPEC's `Out of scope` typing with Future-Flexibility Statusing.** Narrow, local, but high-leverage because it stops the earliest point where `preserve-only` can flatten into `excluded`.
  5. **Sibling long-horizon audit consumer contract for plan-phase.** Reuses the `--reviews` contract shape; makes harness self-improvement routine rather than episodic.
- [d:r:i] Candidates explicitly *not* in the bounded-follow-through set:
  - A `horizon-tension register` as a bare artifact (without consumers). Register would accumulate; no lifecycle surface would read it.
  - A full `future-awareness` section in SPEC (overstated; duplicates CONTEXT).
  - A horizon-watch block crammed into STATE.md (violates STATE's digest scope).
  - A horizon-tension enforcement at auto-advance pipelines (not enough terrain disclosed yet; could harden into ceremony).

### Do Not Collapse Yet

- [d:r:i] **Seeds vs seams.** The distinction matters: seeds are idea-level, operator-authored, trigger-conditional; seams are structural, doctrine-authored, activation-conditional. Collapsing them into one artifact family would weaken both. Keep both open until the seam register lands.
- [d:r:i] **SPEC vs CONTEXT.** The template explicitly separates WHAT/WHY from HOW and future-awareness. Do not move future-awareness into SPEC or back-pressure CONTEXT into requirements-locking. The Future-Flexibility Statusing typing extension to SPEC's Out-of-Scope is the narrow move; anything broader should wait.
- [d:r:i] **Reopen-conditions posture of proposal 28.** Proposal 28 is written as a rule that fires when someone rejects a stronger move on capacity grounds. Turning it into a mechanical gate prematurely would either block legitimate capacity-aware judgment or become ceremonial. Keep it as a review-consumer contract (see bounded candidate 5) rather than a runtime check.
- [d:r:i] **The strength-amplification register vs the current audit lane.** The register's item 1 (explicit strength-amplification register) and this cross-vendor audit lane are the same object under different names. Collapsing them into a durable separate artifact before the audit has produced its routine cadence would harden audit into ceremony.
- [d:r:i] **Multi-dimensional excellence basket.** The register is right to name the dimensions (maintainability, runtime authority clarity, long-horizon carry, operator legibility, auditability, update resilience, intervention yield, cross-vendor reproducibility). Do not turn that basket into a scorecard yet — the point is to prevent silent dominance of one dimension, not to rank them. A rigid scorecard would create new threshold framing (`scored 4.5/5`) exactly where the anti-threshold doctrine is protecting.
- [d:r:i] **Anti-ceremonial pattern check.** It is tempting to add immediate threshold-language linting to SUMMARY / VERIFICATION generation. That should not ship until the gate-language vs doctrine-language distinction is written somewhere durable; otherwise a verify-phase `passed` verdict would itself trigger the lint. Keep this as open terrain until the gate-distinction is explicit.
- [d:r:i] **The register's core framing: positive strengthening vs deficiency healing.** The packet's seed, the spec's framing, and the root AGENTS.md doctrine all converge on `do not treat the harness's task as merely healing deficiency back to adequacy`. That orientation must stay in the register even after specific gaps are closed, because closure pressure alone will pull the program back toward threshold framing. This is the register's most load-bearing non-deficiency claim and should not be collapsed into a checklist.
