## Generic Trigger Rule

- [g:r:i] A preserve-only seam becomes active when at least one of the following happens:
  1. a live planning or implementation artifact now depends on a concrete choice at that seam
  2. a rerun artifact repeatedly improvises around the seam instead of naming it
  3. two or more downstream surfaces would otherwise make inconsistent local choices about the seam
  4. an outward-facing or user-facing surface would silently bind the seam by implementation if doctrine does not bind it first

## Seam Register

| Seam | Current status | Activation trigger | First active landing surface |
|---|---|---|---|
| first low-burden post-private wrapper order | preserve-only seam | the first rerun or follow-on plan needs a concrete sequence for outward wrappers instead of only saying `private first` | product / roadmap steering artifact, not runtime doctrine |
| first bounded audience-right bundle | preserve-only seam | room, controller, or host/player/spectator behavior needs a concrete authority bundle to avoid local improvisation | product / requirements steering artifact |
| later money-family ranking | preserve-only seam | monetization or commercial boundary work becomes a real planning dependency rather than a distant possibility | later product-doctrine lane |
| later host-identity ranking | preserve-only seam | room topology or social authority design now requires choosing how host identity ranks against other session roles | product / room-authority artifact |
| first explicit outward shell | preserve-only seam | external-facing shell work begins and would otherwise silently decide the shell by implementation | product / UX architecture artifact |
| first true event-memory shell | preserve-only seam | repeated-session memory, history, or recap behavior becomes a live product/planning dependency | later product / experience-memory artifact |
| exact cadence taxonomy / first mastery read | preserve-only seam | progression, skill read, or cadence language becomes necessary for content, scoring, or retention planning | later cadence/mastery artifact |
| whether preserve-only contribution/discovery seams ever become active | preserve-only seam | contribution/discovery work stops being hypothetical and starts constraining moderation, topology, or content flow | dedicated contribution/discovery artifact |

## Anti-Misread Rules

- [g:r:i] `Preserve-only` does not mean `never choose`.
- [g:r:i] `Activation trigger` does not mean `choose immediately because someone mentioned it once`.
- [g:r:i] The point is to stop unowned drift between:
  - silent premature binding
  - and permanent non-binding

## Current Disposition

- [d:r:i] No seam is automatically activated by this note alone.
- [d:r:i] The doctrine it introduces is:
  - keep all eight seams explicit
  - require any later artifact that depends on one of them to name that dependency
  - promote the seam from `preserve-only` to an active bounded-open or direct-doctrine surface when the trigger is met
