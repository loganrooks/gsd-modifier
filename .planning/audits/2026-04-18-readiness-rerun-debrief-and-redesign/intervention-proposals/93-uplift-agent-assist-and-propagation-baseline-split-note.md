## Why Agent-Assist Looks Promising Later

- [d:r:i] Project uplift is currently a composition-layer workflow whose detect/write core is helper-driven.
- [d:r:i] That first slice keeps current control concentrated and readable, but later uplift work could still benefit from bounded delegation for:
  - docs/governance uplift classification
  - carrier-gap identification
  - doctrine-sensitive proposal routing
  - cross-runtime or additive-install follow-through packets
- [d:r:i] The promising future shape is not `let an uplift agent absorb the whole problem`.
- [d:r:i] The promising future shape is:
  - main-thread orchestration keeps the composition layer and operator-facing judgment
  - bounded agents take narrower subproblems where the carrier set is explicit and the write scope is auditable

## Why A Baseline Split Looks Promising Later

- [d:r:i] The current layered `v2` propagation family already widened beyond one blended registry.
- [d:r:i] A later stronger form could separate:
  - `upstream-pristine baseline`
    - shipped surface roster and typed upstream relations as they stand before repo-local intervention
  - `repo-local delta map`
    - local-only surfaces
    - local contract additions
    - local consumer widening
    - local carrier mutations
    - change-triggered refresh trail
- [d:r:i] That split would improve:
  - change visibility
  - maintainability
  - portability of repo-local interventions
  - operator control over what is inherited versus what is locally introduced
