## Current Working Decision

## Why The Modifier Route Remains Active

- [d:r:i] The clearest current leverage is still to keep inheriting upstream GSD improvement while carrying local modifier-owned deltas explicitly.
- [d:r:i] A full own-harness route would widen maintenance pressure sharply:
  - installer/materialization ownership
  - workflow and template drift
  - agent/skill/runtime parity
  - future upstream improvement intake
  - release and compatibility burden
- [d:r:i] The modifier route is therefore still the cleaner short-to-medium-horizon path for responsible closure and first deployment pressure.

## Why The Alternative Should Remain Explicit

- [d:r:i] The `own harness` alternative is not fake pressure.
- [d:r:i] It stays earned because:

  - repo extraction pressure is now semantic as well as packaging pressure
  - some local improvements may eventually want deeper control than modifier insertion allows
- [g:r:i] That alternative should remain explicit as a later strategic deliberation, not ambient dissatisfaction or accidental drift.

## Deferral Rule

- [g:r:i] Do not reopen `modifier route versus own harness` as the active decision front before the current responsible-closure sequence sharpens farther.
- [d:r:i] The later strategic deliberation becomes earned after more of these are in hand:
  - the shipped/install contract classification baseline
  - first CI / release-readiness tranche
  - clearer extracted-project boundary
  - first stronger deployability evidence beyond one observe-only host slice
- [d:r:i] Until then, use the modifier route as the working route and judge it by whether it can reach a portable, auditable, deployable extracted project cleanly.
