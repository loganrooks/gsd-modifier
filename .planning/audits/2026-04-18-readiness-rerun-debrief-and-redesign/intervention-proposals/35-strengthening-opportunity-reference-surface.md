# Strengthening Opportunity Reference Surface

## Purpose

- [g:r:i] This surface gives the strengthening family a compact working reference built from the benchmark packet rather than from abstract aspiration alone.
- [d:r:i] It is provisional and revisable. Its authority comes from the benchmark examples in `34`, not from standalone doctrine weight.

## Working Definition

- [g:r:i] A strengthening opportunity is a bounded move that intensifies already-strong terrain, preserves or expands optionality, and has an auditable carry route.

## Minimum Shape

Every strengthening entry should answer:

1. [g:r:i] `Current strength`
   - what is already carrying with real force, so intensification has concrete footing
2. [g:r:i] `Bounded intensification move`
   - what concrete move became visible
3. [g:r:i] `Optionality effect`
   - what this move opens, preserves, or keeps more responsive
4. [g:r:i] `Carry route`
   - `current-phase intensification`
   - or `seeded later resurfacing`

## Route Decision Rule

### Current-Phase Intensification

- [d:r:i] Use this when:
  - the consumer surface already exists
  - the move is bounded enough for the current batch
  - the effect can be made auditable now
- [d:r:i] Benchmark anchors:
  - planning-chain insertion in `32`
  - research/planner contract alignment in `33`

### Seeded Later Resurfacing

- [d:r:i] Use this when:
  - the move is real and bounded
  - but it belongs to a later lifecycle family or wider tranche
  - and it deserves durable resurfacing rather than prose burial
- [d:r:i] Benchmark anchors:
  - future-preservation verification carry in `34`
  - horizon-watch memory carry in `34`

## Strong Positive Signals

- [d:r:i] The move starts from an already-strong surface rather than a blank-slate wish.
- [d:r:i] The move is concrete enough to name as a field, bucket, check, block, or summary.
- [d:r:i] The optionality effect can be stated directly.
- [d:r:i] The route can be audited by a later reader.

## Weak Or Rejectable Forms

- [d:r:i] Generic “improve this later” language.
- [d:r:i] A duplicate open question with no intensification angle.
- [d:r:i] A broad future ambition with no present carrier.
- [d:r:i] A move whose only justification is that it feels richer or more thorough.
