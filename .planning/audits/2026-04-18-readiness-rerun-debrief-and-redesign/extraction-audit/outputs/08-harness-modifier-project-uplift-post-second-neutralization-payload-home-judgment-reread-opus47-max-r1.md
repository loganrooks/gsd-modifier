# Project Uplift Post-Second-Neutralization Payload-Home Judgment

## What The Second Neutralization Tranche Changed

- Lane `06`'s three-surface coupling map is now narrowed. Two of its three still-embedded host surfaces — the host-doctrine carrier catalog and the host-facing rerun-boundary plus skill-command vocabulary — are no longer helper-local constants. `project_uplift.py` reads them through `harness_modifier/uplift/carrier_catalog.py` and `harness_modifier/uplift/vocabulary.py` at analysis entry rather than re-declaring them.
- `STATIC_FILE_CARRIERS` and `MARKER_CARRIERS` are gone from helper code. `file_carrier_specs()` and `marker_carrier_specs()` now build the in-memory `FileCarrierSpec` / `MarkerCarrierSpec` rows out of the typed catalog at `harness_modifier/uplift/carrier_catalog.json`, and the catalog carries `schema_version`, explicit `ordering_rule = "stable_by_key_within_section"`, per-row `group`, `rel_path`, `label`, `fingerprint_shape`, and marker string where relevant. Ten file-carrier rows and four marker-carrier rows are now declared data rather than helper-local host doctrine.
- The `.codex/agents/*.toml` walk in `build_runtime_agent_specs` is no longer hard-coded. The catalog carries a sibling `runtime_agent_registry` object with `key_prefix`, `group`, `rel_path_glob`, `label_prefix`, and `fingerprint_shape`; the helper resolves `runtime_agent_registry_spec()` and parameterizes the glob plus key/label composition off that row. The enumerate-vs-expand distinction is preserved as a named sibling rather than hidden inside `file_carriers`.
- `RERUN_BOUNDARY_PATTERNS`, `SEED_MIGRATION_SKILL_COMMAND`, and the `$gsd-uplift-project` strings are no longer literal helper constants. `vocabulary.json` now carries four pattern regexes (`pre-rerun`, `fresh discuss \+ plan required`, `rerun-boundary`, `input to the next discuss pass`), four skill-command entries (`uplift_detect_only`, `uplift_write`, `seed_migration_inventory`, `seed_migration_write`), five phase-boundary note strings, and ten operator-facing recommendation sentences keyed by report-, state-, and progress-note surface. Pattern compilation stays helper-side under `_compiled_rerun_boundary_patterns()`, so compilation flags do not leak into the JSON.
- The state-heading plus recommendation strings now route through `recommendation_text(...)` and `state_heading()` / `output_policy.json`, so the state-section body is a rendering template fed by typed tokens rather than an f-string wrapping embedded skill-namespace literals. Both the raw tokens and the full operator-facing sentences live in the vocabulary carrier, not only the tokens.
- `seed_migration_inventory.py` and `harness_canary.py` continue to read the same typed seed-contract and output-policy surfaces directly. The second tranche did not widen their consumption, but it also did not re-introduce a helper-constant pathway those consumers would have to thread around.
- `tooling/codex/tests/test_project_uplift.py` now directly covers the sharper frontier: loader shape, canonical section ordering, absent-additive stability across a no-op catalog reorder, doctrine-reference-hash stability across a no-op catalog reorder, carrier-fingerprint stability across a no-op catalog reorder, and recommendation plus drift-reason equivalence across a no-op catalog reorder. Carrier and vocabulary externalization is tied down against silent classification drift.
- The overlay governance refresh has tracked this: `harness_modifier/overlay/helpers/AUTHORITY-MAP.md` now names both landed tranches explicitly and narrows the blocker to reopened payload-home judgment; `harness_modifier/overlay/ROSTER.md` now says the next helper question is post-second-neutralization payload-home judgment rather than carrier extraction.

## Remaining Host Or Shared-Boundary Coupling

- Planning-state writer reach. `state_status`, `latest_phase_context_path`, `count_phase_files`, and `update_state_section` still operate directly on `.planning/STATE.md` and `.planning/phases/*/*-CONTEXT.md`. `update_state_section` in particular (i) rewrites a section keyed by the typed `state_heading` under `## Project Uplift`, (ii) inserts that section above host-specific sibling markers `## Deferred Items` or `## Session Continuity`, and (iii) appends to a trailing state file if neither marker is present. The anchor heading is typed, but the sibling-marker choice and insertion algorithm are helper-local knowledge of this host's STATE.md topology.
- Planning-state section body composition. `state_section_text` still builds the body out of f-string lines with literal labels (`Last uplift pass:`, `Last uplift class:`, `Phase boundary signal:`, `Compatibility posture:`, `Compatibility declaration:`, `Observed runtime basis:`, `Held runtime annotation:`, `Seed corpus posture:`, `Pending doctrine-sensitive proposals:`, `Current recommendation:`, `Current uplift report:`, `Current uplift manifest:`). These labels act as a state-section schema of this host's planning surface that has not been typed yet; the second tranche typed the heading and the operator-facing sentences inside it but not the line-level labels.
- Phase-activity shape assumptions. `phase_sort_key` splits the parent directory name on the first `-` and treats the prefix as either a numeric (`0`, `72`, `72.1`) or string-prefixed phase key. `count_phase_files(repo_root, "*/*-PLAN.md")` and `count_phase_files(repo_root, "*/*-SUMMARY.md")` rely on that same `.planning/phases/NN[.M]-slug/NN[.M]-FILE.md` layout. That layout is this host's phase-numbering shape and is not declared anywhere yet.
- Install-contract pointer. `OVERLAY_MANIFEST_REL_PATH = "tooling/portable-gsd/overlay/OVERLAY-MANIFEST.json"` is still helper-local and still directly couples the helper to the current install-target wiring. Lane `07` explicitly left this out of the second tranche as install-contract wiring rather than host-doctrine or operator-facing vocabulary; that classification holds but the coupling itself is unchanged.
- Held-later family home inside the helper's own source tree. `held_later_rel_path` resolves through `output_policy.json` to `tooling/codex/UPLIFT-HELD-LATER.md`. The pointer is typed, but the artifact home sits inside the helper's own directory rather than under `.planning/` or `harness_modifier/uplift/`. The helper reads that file as a text catalog of held-later families; the file itself is host-repo-local.
- Downstream-helper tails. `seed_migration_inventory.py` still imports `project_uplift` for shared readers and now also reads the seed-contract carrier directly, and `harness_canary.py` still calls `pu.build_progress_note(repo_root)` for its uplift-compatibility check. Both consumers travel only after their own host-coupling tails are themselves neutralized; neither is cleared to lead.

## Payload-Home Judgment Now

- The payload boundary is materially thinner than the lane-`06` boundary. The host-doctrine catalog surface and the host-facing vocabulary surface — the two surfaces lane `06` said had to move before payload-home was reopenable as relocation — are now typed carriers rather than helper-local constants, and the helper consumes them at analysis entry through loader modules that sit beside the existing compatibility and output-policy loaders.
- The payload boundary is still not a clean relocation boundary. One of lane `06`'s three named host-coupling surfaces is still embedded in helper code: the `.planning/STATE.md` writer and `.planning/phases/*/*-CONTEXT.md` scanner reach, plus the host-specific sibling-marker knowledge inside `update_state_section`, plus the phase-directory shape assumption inside `phase_sort_key` and `count_phase_files`. Relocating the payload now would carry a writer that embeds knowledge of this host's planning-state topology — `## Project Uplift` slotted next to `## Deferred Items` and `## Session Continuity`, under a `.planning/STATE.md` layout anchored to this repo's shape — into whatever the new home is, which re-hosts the same planning-shape blur one move outward instead of dissolving it.
- The install-contract pointer tail (`OVERLAY_MANIFEST_REL_PATH`) is a second, narrower coupling but sits in a different class. It is install-contract wiring, not host-doctrine content or operator-facing vocabulary, so it has its own neutralization route and does not need to ride with the planning-shape tranche.
- The cleaner next adjacent route is therefore not relocation, not a pivot to a different extraction family (second overlay tranche, overwrite-family widening, standalone repo, packaging), and not a narrower payload-home preparation that assumes relocation is the next move. It is the host-planning-shape neutralization slice that lane `06` and lane `07` already put on deck: externalize the state-writer plus phase-scanner plus the `state_section_text` label schema into typed carriers with a separate writer surface, keeping the helper's classification and rendering algorithms intact.
- Ordering inside the held-later stack matters. Host-planning-shape neutralization should lead because it is the remaining one of lane `06`'s three named host-coupling surfaces and because it materially changes what any later relocation would have to carry. Install-contract pointer neutralization is its own narrower later slice after that, so it does not silently widen the planning-shape tranche. Only after both of those tranches plus their propagation refreshes land should the payload-home question be reopened a third time on a further-thinned helper.
- `seed_migration_inventory.py` stays downstream; it still imports `pu` and cannot lead payload-home movement. `harness_canary.py` stays a neighboring consumer that reads the output-policy carrier plus `pu.build_progress_note`; it does not inherit payload-home authority. `audit_refmap.py` stays out of the later payload-movement candidate set per the helper authority map. `.codex` remains observed basis and `.claude` remains held annotation; the observation carrier's candidate runtimes stay candidate rather than riding in as parity expansion.
