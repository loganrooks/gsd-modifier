# Project Uplift Neutralization Reread

## What The Current Proposal Already Clarified

- The proposal keeps the bounded object opened by `153` narrow: the next move is policy neutralization of `tooling/codex/project_uplift.py`, not payload relocation, not a second overlay tranche, not overwrite-family source split, not standalone repo or npm/`npx` packaging, and not a second-host exercise. Lane `04`'s per-helper classification carries unchanged into that bound: `project_uplift.py` is the modifier-facing payload candidate that must earn its next slice; `seed_migration_inventory.py` is a strictly downstream consumer; `audit_refmap.py` is out of the family altogether.
- The proposal names three distinct neutralization targets rather than one umbrella target, and the three live at different seams: `RUNTIME_DIRS` is a *discovery* surface, the uplift output/path constants are an *emit* surface, and the compatibility-declaration globals are a *consume* surface. Keeping those three seams separate is the most important thing `153` already bought, because any flattening into "one config file" would re-hide the authority split that lane `04` just made visible.
- The proposal keeps the runtime horizon at `.codex` observed basis plus `.claude` held annotation via `harness_modifier/compatibility/declaration.json`. No cross-runtime composition, no widened provider matrix, and no `.claude` install/materialization widening get reopened as conditions of this slice.
- The proposal keeps payload ownership stable during neutralization. The live helper remains at `tooling/codex/project_uplift.py`, the overlay shim under `harness_modifier/overlay/helpers/` keeps its bridge role, and the current output topology (`.planning/UPLIFT-REPORT.md`, `.planning/UPLIFT-MANIFEST.json`, `STATE.md` `## Project Uplift` section, `tooling/codex/UPLIFT-HELD-LATER.md`) stays in place. The slice re-homes *how* policy is expressed, not *where* artifacts land.
- The proposal carries the downstream consumer forward explicitly: `seed_migration_inventory.py` reaches `project_uplift` as `pu` for both plain parsing helpers and seed-contract shape constants, so neutralization has to distinguish helper reuse (keep) from policy re-export through a helper namespace (discharge).

## Runtime Discovery Judgment

- The posture to carry is that `RUNTIME_DIRS` is an *observation* surface, not a *declaration* surface. It answers "which runtime directories are worth looking at on disk?" — a question whose answer should follow from the compatibility declaration's `runtime_basis.runtime` plus `runtime_held_annotations[*].runtime`, not from a helper-local seven-entry list that silently carries multi-provider appetite (`.gemini`, `.config/opencode`, `.opencode`, `.config/kilo`, `.kilo`) without any upstream declaration authority.
- The helper already emits cross-runtime secondary signals off that list: `secondary_signals` appends `cross_runtime` whenever a non-`.codex` entry is present, and `classify_project` returns `cross-runtime uplift` on the same condition. The list materially shapes classification output. Leaving it as a module constant means a latent multi-provider policy is deciding `cross_runtime` labeling without traveling through the compatibility declaration that otherwise governs runtime basis.
- The neutralization move is to derive the observation set from declaration data rather than to widen the declaration to match today's ambient list. The clean target shape is a declaration-side field — `observed_runtime_directories` as an ordered list derived from `runtime_basis.runtime` plus every `runtime_held_annotations[*].runtime` — and a separate, explicitly named `candidate_observed_runtime_directories` field for the `.gemini` / `.opencode` / `.kilo` entries that have no upstream declaration anchor yet. The helper then reads both fields as data and keeps detection behavior stable while the extra provider appetite is made visible rather than implicit.
- This split preserves `.codex` observed basis and `.claude` held annotation exactly; it refuses to pretend the five speculative entries are also observed basis; and it does not re-argue multi-provider composition inside this slice, because the candidate-vs-observed split is a *typing* of what the helper-local list was already silently doing.
- The discovery carrier should live next to the compatibility declaration rather than on its own island. Two viable shapes: either extend `harness_modifier/compatibility/declaration.json` with the two new fields and let `declaration.py` expose them, or land a sibling typed carrier under `harness_modifier/compatibility/` (for example `observation.json` + `observation.py`) that declares the observation surface while leaving the compatibility declaration's current schema and version untouched. The adjacent-sibling route is the cleaner first cut because it avoids bumping the compatibility declaration's `schema_version` and keeps parity-baseline semantics frozen; the single-carrier route can be re-chosen later if the observation surface proves to be a strict function of runtime basis.

## Uplift Output And Path Judgment

- The eleven path-and-shape constants in `project_uplift.py` are not one policy bucket; they split into four distinct classes, and the neutralization slice earns cleaner results by naming the classes rather than flattening them:
  - **Uplift output topology** — `STATE_HEADING`, `REPORT_REL_PATH`, `MANIFEST_REL_PATH`, and the consumer-input `HELD_LATER_REL_PATH`. These are the uplift memory shape the helper owns as producer. They belong together in a typed output-policy carrier because any relocation or later consumer widening needs them as a coherent set.
  - **Runtime-observation input paths** — `RUNTIME_VERSION_REL_PATH` and `RUNTIME_MANIFEST_REL_PATH`. These duplicate information the compatibility declaration already carries as `runtime_basis.version_source` and `runtime_basis.manifest_version_source`. Keeping them as module constants is pure policy duplication; the neutralization is to read them from the declaration at call time and delete the constants.
  - **Install-target input path** — `OVERLAY_MANIFEST_REL_PATH` points at the install-target authority identified in lane `04`. This is not uplift-output policy and it is not compatibility-declaration policy; it belongs to a later install-target accessor that can be named but should not expand the scope of this slice. Hold in place behind a named accessor and mark it as not-this-slice.
  - **Seed-contract shape** — `SEED_DIR_REL_PATH`, `CURRENT_SEED_CONTRACT_VERSION`, `REQUIRED_SEED_FRONTMATTER_KEYS`, and `REQUIRED_SEED_SECTION_HEADINGS`. These constants are doctrine that spans the `project_uplift` / `seed_migration_inventory` pair: the downstream helper currently reaches them through `pu.CURRENT_SEED_CONTRACT_VERSION`, `pu.REQUIRED_SEED_FRONTMATTER_KEYS`, and `pu.REQUIRED_SEED_SECTION_HEADINGS`. Leaving them as `project_uplift`-local constants makes the producer helper the implicit owner of seed-contract doctrine, which is exactly the "helper-local hidden policy" shape this lane is discharging.
- The right neutralization shape is therefore **two carriers, not one**, and not a single undifferentiated config bucket:
  - a typed **uplift output policy** carrier (covering output topology + held-later input) that travels with `project_uplift.py` into any later relocation,
  - a typed **seed contract shape** carrier that governs both `project_uplift.py` and `seed_migration_inventory.py` and lives next to the compatibility/observation carriers rather than inside the uplift helper's namespace.
- Splitting this way carries three concrete wins. It gives the downstream consumer a declaration-owned authority route (seed_migration_inventory reads seed-shape from a typed carrier, not from `pu.X` globals). It keeps uplift output policy portable as one object when later relocation is re-evaluated. And it leaves the install-target path and the duplicated runtime-observation paths visible as separate routing questions rather than dissolving them into the same file.
- The slice should not change today's output topology — the `.planning/UPLIFT-*` locations, the `STATE.md` `## Project Uplift` heading, and the `tooling/codex/UPLIFT-HELD-LATER.md` held-later file all stay exactly where they are. The neutralization is in *where the policy is expressed*, not in the topology itself.

## Compatibility Routing Judgment

- The helper currently materializes the declaration into nine module-level globals at import time (`COMPATIBILITY_DECLARATION`, `COMPATIBILITY_DECLARATION_REL_PATH`, `UPLIFT_MANIFEST_SCHEMA_VERSION`, `COMPATIBILITY_POSTURE`, `RUNTIME_BASIS_DECLARATION`, `RUNTIME_HELD_ANNOTATIONS_DECLARATION`, `DECLARED_OVERLAY_SCHEMA_VERSION`, `UPSTREAM_COMPATIBILITY_WINDOW`, `PARITY_SCAN_BASELINE_DECLARATION`, `COMPATIBILITY_CHECK_PROTOCOL`, `COMPATIBILITY_HELD_LATER`). Those globals convert declaration authority into helper-local symbolic authority: downstream code — including `seed_migration_inventory.py` — can reach declaration semantics through the helper's namespace rather than through the declaration module, and any evolution of the declaration has to simultaneously preserve the helper's global surface. That is the copying posture `153` is asking to thin.
- The neutralization move is to stop letting the helper re-export declaration semantics. The concrete shape:
  - remove the module-level globals; load the declaration (or a thin typed accessor over it) inside `analyze_repo` and `build_progress_note` on demand,
  - pass that loaded declaration (or the accessor) explicitly through `build_compatibility_basis`, `held_runtime_annotation`, `compatibility_drift_reasons`, and anywhere else today's module-global chain is consumed,
  - let `seed_migration_inventory.py` keep importing plain parsing helpers from `pu` (`read_text`, `parse_frontmatter_map`, `frontmatter_text`, `extract_h2_headings`, `parse_seed_contract_version`) because those are implementation, not policy; but migrate its seed-shape references off `pu.CURRENT_SEED_CONTRACT_VERSION` / `pu.REQUIRED_SEED_FRONTMATTER_KEYS` / `pu.REQUIRED_SEED_SECTION_HEADINGS` onto the new seed-contract shape carrier.
- The declaration keeps its current schema, its current `compatibility_posture: observed_basis_only`, its current `runtime_basis` plus `runtime_held_annotations` split, its current `check_protocol`, and its current `held_later` list. The slice does not widen `upstream_compatibility_window`, does not touch `parity_scan_baseline`, and does not reopen anything the declaration holds as later. The thinner consumer posture is the whole move; nothing in the declaration itself changes.
- One derived win is worth naming explicitly: once the helper consumes rather than re-exports, later relocation of the payload into `harness_modifier/` is no longer gated on also rewriting every module-global reference. That removes a hidden coupling that would otherwise make the relocation slice heavier than it needs to be, without committing to relocation here.

## What Should Stay Helper-Local

- The regex heuristics — `RERUN_BOUNDARY_PATTERNS` in particular. Rerun-boundary recognition is a detection algorithm whose shape may evolve; it is not upstream-declared authority and does not belong in typed data at this slice.
- The UI label strings — `PROGRESS_NOTE_RENDER_FIELDS`, `PROGRESS_NOTE_REASON_LABEL`, `SEED_POSTURE_REASON_LABEL`, `SEED_MIGRATION_CANDIDATE_LABEL`, `SEED_MIGRATION_BREAKDOWN_LABEL`, `SEED_MIGRATION_INSPECT_POINTER_LABEL`, `SEED_MIGRATION_WRITE_POINTER_LABEL`. - The carrier spec lists — `STATIC_FILE_CARRIERS` and `MARKER_CARRIERS`, plus the `FileCarrierSpec` and `MarkerCarrierSpec` dataclasses, plus `build_runtime_agent_specs`. These *are* real hidden policy (they name which doctrine-sensitive reference surfaces the uplift helper watches), but moving them into typed data is a substantially larger slice than the three targets `153` names, and doing it here would bundle too much together and blur the bounded neutralization object. - The skill-command strings `SEED_MIGRATION_SKILL_COMMAND` and `SEED_MIGRATION_WRITE_COMMAND`. They trace back to the operator-facing pointer bridge in `91/92`; treating them as helper-local keeps the bridge posture stable and avoids opening an adjacent bridge-home question inside this slice.
