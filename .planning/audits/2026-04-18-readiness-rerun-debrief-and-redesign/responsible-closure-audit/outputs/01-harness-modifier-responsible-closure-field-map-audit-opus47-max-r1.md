## Discrepancy, Positive Gain, And Semantic Signal Capture

- [d:r:i] Discrepancy carriers should distinguish at least four vectors so later review does not collapse them into one "broken" bucket:
  - `contract-mismatch` — declaration asserted X, host observed Y (compatibility-window violation, schema-version mismatch, basis outside declared support)
  - `overlay-authority-drift` — modifier-owned source ships one shape, install-target mirror diverges (catches manifest/source desync the authority split from [148](../../intervention-proposals/148-harness-modifier-first-overlay-filesystem-rehome-implementation.md) is specifically designed to surface)
  - `refmap/topology drift` — `audit_refmap.py verify` fails after a concurrent lane wrote paths the frozen basis assumed
  - `parity-classifier drift` — `.codex` vs held-annotation `.claude` observed posture diverges against the parity_scan_baseline
- [d:r:i] Positive-gain carriers should be a first-class class, not a residual of "no discrepancy detected." Concrete examples:
  - an `externalize` tranche closed a helper-local literal and now the declaration carries it (evidence: the literal count at `tooling/codex/project_uplift.py` thinned from N to 0 across `154` / `157` / `159`)
  - a `propagate` pass updated producer / consumer / registry / governance surfaces coherently (evidence: the propagation registry v2 rows stayed in sync after the slice)
  - a `neutralize` tranche removed a host-local embed (evidence: the `.planning/STATE.md` slot shape is now read through a typed carrier rather than a helper constant)
- [d:r:i] Semantic signal capture should record what shape the observation broadened, sharpened, intensified, or made more durable — not whether it passed a threshold. This is the anti-threshold posture in telemetry form, and it is the axis GSD Reflect's severity / polarity fields do not directly carry.
- [d:r:i] Measurement-provenance carriers should govern the observation itself:
  - attach `detected_by` and `written_by` signature objects per [commands/gsd/signal.md](/home/rookslog/workspace/projects/get-shit-done-reflect/commands/gsd/signal.md) — even when they resolve to the same runtime facts — so manual vs lane-driven origins stay distinguishable later
  - record `about_work` (artifact/work under judgment) rather than leaving it as `[]` when the conversation identifies it, so discrepancy and positive-gain observations have explicit subjects
  - fall back to `not_available` rather than invention when runtime, model, session, or version are unknown
