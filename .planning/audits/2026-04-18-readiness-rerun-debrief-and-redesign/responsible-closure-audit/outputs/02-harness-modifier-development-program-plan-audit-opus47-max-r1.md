# Harness Modifier Development Program Plan Audit

## Overall Judgment

- [g:r:i] Disposition: `accept with revisions`. `166` is the right shape for the next integrated bounded object in the harness-modifier development program. It gathers responsible closure, development-side parallelization, intervention lifecycle discipline, horizons, task-type routing, and actual/virtual intervention surfaces into one carrier without collapsing them, and it keeps deployment-readiness and harness-in-action workflow rewrites out of frame.
- [g:r:i] The revisions are bounded and carry-focused, not a rewrite. The plan's weakest axis is not its structure; it is that it does not yet inherit by name the durable taxonomies already frozen in adjacent lane `01` returns (six-surface split, five-family signal taxonomy, three-class parallelization taxonomy). Without that named inheritance, later consumers will reconstruct those distinctions ad hoc from chat memory or from route note `161` alone, which is exactly what closure lane `01` was trying to prevent.
- [d:r:i] Anti-misread fences the plan must keep (and this audit must keep):
  - [d:r:i] do not let `accept with revisions` collapse into a ship-gate verdict; the plan is a protocol-pressure carrier, not a deployment object
  - [d:r:i] do not let the revisions list become a single implementation queue; they are per-carrier sharpenings with their own ownership
  - [d:r:i] do not demand harness-in-action workflow rewrites from this plan; those stay protected-future carries with their own triggering basis
  - [d:r:i] do not flatten actual and possible intervention surfaces into one inventory; their distinct treatment is part of why `166` is the right next object

- [d:r:i] The actual/virtual intervention-surface split is preserved as two distinct sections with different stances. - [d:r:i] The four-workstream shape (A: dev operations and protocol; B: responsible closure and deployability; C: propagation and verification; D: intervention-surface expansion) partitions ownership cleanly and keeps the current-status line per stream honest (`under-carried`, `first field map completed`, `field map exists but parallelization-adjacent consequences uneven`, `actual partly visible, virtual not yet governed strongly`). - [d:r:i] The `Exact Next Moves` ordering — audit this plan cross-vendor before cutting the next protocol tranche; pair protocol slice with targeted propagation audit on already-landed parallelization-adjacent workflow changes; keep harness-in-action parallelization explicit-but-later — preserves the parallelization internal cross-audit's protocol-pressure recommendation and the closure lane `01`'s pre-deployment sequencing.
