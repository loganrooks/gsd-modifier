# Harness Maximal Improvement Field Map Packet

## Purpose

- [g:r:i] This packet scopes a bounded full-field widening lane over how the repo-local GSD harness can still become more maintainable, more robust, more update-resilient, more operator-legible, and more future-carrying.
- [g:r:i] The task is not to decide whether the harness is already good enough. The task is to map where the current form could intensify further and where the present families still leave opportunity on the table.

## Governing Questions

1. [d:r:i] Across the current harness-intervention, uplift, propagation, threshold, governance, and long-horizon families, where does current carry still thin or remain fragmented?
2. [d:r:i] Which improvement pressures cluster together across several surfaces rather than belonging to only one local family?
3. [d:r:i] Which kinds of strengthening are still underrepresented:
   - docs/governance carry
   - workflow/skill/reference carry
   - helper/test/tooling carry
   - compatibility/update carry
   - lifecycle carry
   - operator-control / relevance-routing carry
   - harness self-improvement carry
4. [d:r:i] Which bounded future interventions appear once the full field is mapped, even if they were not yet named explicitly?

## Anti-Misread Rules

- [g:r:i] Do not frame the task as `is the harness adequate`, `is it ready`, `what is the top few`, or any similar narrowing/gating question.
- [g:r:i] Do not flatten distinct improvement dimensions into one score or one verdict.
- [g:r:i] Map the field first. Only after that, name bounded intervention families or sequences.
- [g:r:i] Do not assume that currently active families are already the whole field.

## Desired Output Shape

- [d:r:i] A full-field map that includes:
  - current strengthening families and where they already carry force
  - where carry still thins, fragments, or remains weakly consumed
  - omitted or undernamed improvement families
  - cross-family clusters and tensions
  - bounded future intervention families surfaced by the full map
