This chunk begins from a very practical problem. Once the note has admitted that earlier availability comes in degrees, the debrief can no longer speak about every case as though it were the same kind of miss. The proposed vocabulary is sharp: `explicit miss`, `traceable undercarry`, `retrospective possibility`. These are not only descriptive differences. They are epistemic markers for how strong the continuity is and therefore how strong the backward indictment can responsibly be. The strength of this move is that it lets critique become more exact without becoming evasive.

But the note does not let the vocabulary settle too quickly. Full stabilization would turn a nuance-preserving distinction into another rigid scheme. Total informality would let the distinctions evaporate. So the vocabulary is given a deliberately intermediate status: explicit enough for repeated use, light enough to be revised, framed as an aid to judgment rather than a sovereign taxonomy. This is an important discipline because it keeps the framework from solving one blunt register by creating a new bureaucratic one.

That is why the next two sections ask what strain in the vocabulary would actually look like. Not every hard case proves failure. Edge pressure can be productive. Genuine strain appears when the terms start obscuring treatment-relevant differences, when one label covers materially different continuity levels, when cases fit only by interpretive force, when moral valence outruns epistemic support, or when rival readers disagree because the terms are underdefined rather than because the case is genuinely contestable. The repair logic is equally careful. Sometimes the right repair is a split. Sometimes it is stronger boundary justification. Sometimes it is a new annotation lane. Sometimes the problem is not the terms but mechanical use, and the repair has to be procedural rather than taxonomic. The note is trying to keep the vocabulary alive under pressure rather than defended by habit.

The most important turn then comes when the note asks what these labels should actually change. If the labels remain epistemically finer than practice, the framework has gained nuance without consequences. The answer is that the labels should alter tone of critique, burden of proof, likely remedy, and degree of rerun or redesign pressure. An `explicit miss` licenses the sharpest backward critique. A `traceable undercarry` supports a critique of weak translation, thin carry-forward, or missing carriers. A `retrospective possibility` carries the lightest indictment but can still justify serious forward-looking design attention. This is a strong step because it couples description to action without pretending the label alone decides the whole response.

Immediately after that, the note places a brake on itself. Continuity strength alone is not enough to determine consequence. Practical response also depends on consequence profile: downstream force, practical cost, reversibility, recurrence, repeated compensation, endangered futures or seams. The note's most useful distinction here is between epistemic force of the classification and practical force of the response. Those two axes inform one another, but they do not collapse. A strong continuity claim can still warrant a bounded response if the case was low-force and reversible. A weaker continuity claim can still demand serious design attention if the future consequences are large. This is exactly the kind of anti-mechanical clarification the vocabulary needed.

The response-matrix sections complete the turn. The note wants a matrix, but not a score. It should be guide-shaped rather than score-shaped: no totals, no automatic verdicts, no composite seriousness ladder. Instead it should surface recurring response families and require explanation where a case departs from the usual band. That is why response bands are named by primary action families such as `archival mention`, `future-preserving branch`, `targeted carry-forward repair`, `direct rerun scrutiny`, `structural redesign pressure`, then qualified separately by retrospective weight and prospective pressure. The crucial point is that the qualifiers must remain axis-local. They answer different questions. They must not become the real hidden score under another name.

This matters because mixed profiles are exactly what a hidden score would erase. Light retrospective weight with high prospective pressure. Strong retrospective weight with only moderate practical force. Those mixtures are not noise in the framework. They are what the framework exists to preserve. The note ends the slice by making that explicit: qualifiers remain useful only if they keep ambiguity visible instead of resolving it prematurely. That is the through-line of the whole chunk. A vocabulary sharp enough to matter, light enough to revise, consequential enough to change remedy, and disciplined enough not to become a concealed ladder of seriousness.

This chunk therefore turns the underreach problem into a response grammar. Different strengths of continuity need different labels, but those labels stay provisional. They need repair under strain. They need practical consequences, yet they cannot become automatic engines of response. The framework needs response bands, yet not scores. It needs qualifiers, yet not composite seriousness. If I extend the point, I would say that the slice is trying to build a vocabulary that can sharpen judgment without replacing it: a language of underreach whose real test is whether it changes critique and remedy while still preserving mixed cases as mixed. That phrasing is mine, but it follows the sequence closely.

# Operational Translation

## Graded Underreach Vocabulary

Textual pressure: one accusatory register is too coarse for different strengths of prior availability.
Interpretive translation: `explicit miss`, `traceable undercarry`, and `retrospective possibility` mark different continuity strengths and therefore different strengths of critique.
Audit-design implication: classify underreach claims using a graded vocabulary that explicitly distinguishes direct carry-forward failure from weaker reconstruction.
Scope: continuity assessment and critique calibration.
Confidence: high.

## Provisional Stabilization

Textual pressure: the vocabulary needs repeatability without freezing into doctrine.
Interpretive translation: the right status is explicit but revisable.
Audit-design implication: define and reuse the vocabulary across cases while keeping it open to splitting, revision, or procedural supplementation when strain appears.
Scope: framework language and method maintenance.
Confidence: high.

## Vocabulary Strain Diagnostics

Textual pressure: not every edge case shows failure, but some do.
Interpretive translation: genuine strain appears when the terms stop clarifying treatment-relevant differences or when moral tone outruns evidentiary support.
Audit-design implication: watch for same-label/different-continuity collapse, forced fit, missing distinctions, and repeated underdefinition disagreement.
Scope: method self-audit and taxonomy repair.
Confidence: high.

## Repair Ordering

Textual pressure: strained vocabularies can be misrepaired by reflexive proliferation or reflexive parsimony.
Interpretive translation: repair depends on the kind of misclassification involved.
Audit-design implication: first ask whether the strain is local or structural, then whether the problem is coarse terms, weak boundary discipline, or a missing distinction, and only then choose split, annotation, stronger justification, or procedural repair.
Scope: taxonomy maintenance and review design.
Confidence: high.

## Labels That Change Practice

Textual pressure: labels are empty if they do not alter critique or remedy.
Interpretive translation: continuity labels should regulate tone, burden of proof, remedy family, and rerun/redesign pressure.
Audit-design implication: tie `explicit miss` to strongest backward critique, `traceable undercarry` to translation/carrier repair, and `retrospective possibility` to lighter indictment with potentially serious future-design attention.
Scope: audit writing and remedy selection.
Confidence: high.

## Epistemic Force Versus Practical Force

Textual pressure: continuity strength alone does not determine how much response is warranted.
Interpretive translation: the evidentiary strength of the classification and the downstream consequence profile are related but distinct axes.
Audit-design implication: combine continuity classification with consequence profile rather than letting labels automatically trigger maximal or minimal response.
Scope: proportional response design.
Confidence: high.

## Guide-Shaped Response Matrix

Textual pressure: the framework needs visible response logic without collapsing into scoring.
Interpretive translation: a good matrix surfaces recurring response families rather than computing verdicts.
Audit-design implication: use prose response bands with default treatments and required override explanation, not numbers or composite seriousness scores.
Scope: action guidance and audit decision support.
Confidence: high.

## Axis-Local Qualifiers

Textual pressure: qualifiers can quietly become the real scoring system.
Interpretive translation: retrospective weight and prospective pressure must remain separate descriptors answering different questions.
Audit-design implication: present qualifiers as axis-local, forbid composite rollups, and require prose explanation where mixed profiles matter.
Scope: presentation design and anti-metric safeguards.
Confidence: high.

# Workflow Translation

## Continuity-Calibrated Debrief Writing

Inferential step: because different underreach strengths warrant different tones, workflow should bind critique style to continuity classification.
Workflow consequence: debrief notes should state whether a case is an explicit miss, traceable undercarry, or retrospective possibility and let that classification change accusation level, burden placement, and remedy style.
Scope: debrief authoring and review tone.
Confidence: high.

## Vocabulary Maintenance Pass

Inferential step: because a graded vocabulary can strain over time, workflow should periodically test whether it still sharpens real distinctions.
Workflow consequence: framework reviews should inspect where the terms are clarifying repeated cases, where they are forcing fit, and whether repair should be taxonomic or procedural.
Scope: framework upkeep, audit method review.
Confidence: high.

## Response-Band Guidance

Inferential step: because labels alone do not determine response, workflow should route cases through a guide-shaped response matrix.
Workflow consequence: case handling should pair continuity strength with consequence profile and then select among action families such as archival mention, future-preserving branch, targeted carry-forward repair, direct rerun scrutiny, or structural redesign pressure.
Scope: response planning, rerun governance, design follow-up.
Confidence: high.

## Anti-Scoring Presentation Rule

Inferential step: because qualifiers can harden into a hidden score, workflow should preserve mixed cases as mixed.
Workflow consequence: review artifacts should avoid points, totals, thresholds, or composite seriousness labels and instead keep retrospective weight and prospective pressure separate with prose explanation when they pull apart.
Scope: report design, matrix presentation, decision support.
Confidence: high.
