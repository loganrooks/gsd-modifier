This chunk asks the practical question that follows any guarded hypothesis: when does it become a research obligation? The note rejects a very common mistake here. Future relevance is not enough. A research obligation emerges only from a configuration of stakes, reversibility, opportunity cost of delay, decision dependence, and tractability. This is a useful test because it turns obligation away from mere interestingness and toward unresolved consequence. The question becomes whether leaving the issue weakly warranted would materially distort later action or foreclose options.

That leads directly to the next clarification: not every obligation deserves a full research lane. The note offers three forms instead. A full lane is for upstream, high-stakes, tractable questions. A bounded question packet is for narrower uncertainties that block a specific discrimination. A trigger-conditioned inquiry debt entry is for questions that matter, but not enough or not yet enough to justify immediate inquiry. This is one of the sharpest procedural distinctions in the tail because it keeps research obligation proportional instead of maximal by default.

The last section then places the real burden on deferred inquiry. Inquiry debt is not real unless its trigger is concrete enough to alter future behavior. `When this becomes relevant` is not a trigger. A real debt entry names the question, states the concrete trigger, identifies the affected decision surface, and indicates who or what should notice it. The note is right to say that the deeper issue is not memory but future interruptibility. A good debt entry changes what the system will later notice. A bad one merely records that someone once had a concern.

This chunk therefore makes inquiry itself answerable to consequence and routing. Some weak claims demand research; others demand only restraint. Some research obligations justify full lanes; others only bounded packets or deferred debt. And deferred debt becomes real only when its future wake condition is sharp enough to change later behavior. If I extend the point, I would say that the slice is trying to prevent two equal failures: the inflation of every unresolved question into a research program and the burial of consequential uncertainty in a respectable archive of vague future notes. That phrasing is mine, but it follows the sequence closely.

# Operational Translation

## Research Obligation As Unresolved Consequence

Textual pressure: uncertainty plus interest is too weak a basis for opening inquiry.
Interpretive translation: research obligation arises when weakly warranted uncertainty materially threatens later decisions, reversibility, or option preservation.
Audit-design implication: judge research obligation by stakes, reversibility, opportunity cost, decision dependence, and tractability rather than future relevance alone.
Scope: inquiry triage and claim follow-up.
Confidence: high.

## Three Inquiry Forms

Textual pressure: not every real obligation warrants a full research lane.
Interpretive translation: inquiry should be shaped proportionately.
Audit-design implication: distinguish full research lanes, bounded question packets, and trigger-conditioned inquiry debt entries according to stakes and scope.
Scope: research design and workflow routing.
Confidence: high.

## Actionable Inquiry Debt

Textual pressure: deferred inquiry often becomes a respectable way of forgetting.
Interpretive translation: inquiry debt is real only when it contains a concrete wake condition and a future decision surface.
Audit-design implication: require debt entries to name the question, trigger condition, affected decision, and expected noticing surface or actor.
Scope: future-aware planning and research deferral.
Confidence: high.

# Workflow Translation

## Research-Obligation Gate

Inferential step: because not every uncertainty merits a lane, workflow should gate inquiry by consequence.
Workflow consequence: before opening research, reviewers should ask what decision depends on the uncertainty, how reversible action is, what delay would cost, and whether inquiry can meaningfully reduce the uncertainty now.
Scope: research triage and planning.
Confidence: high.

## Inquiry-Form Selection

Inferential step: because obligations vary in scale, workflow should choose the smallest honest inquiry form.
Workflow consequence: route major upstream uncertainties to full lanes, narrow blockers to bounded packets, and deferred but real questions to trigger-conditioned inquiry debt.
Scope: process routing and research planning.
Confidence: high.

## Debt Entry Validation

Inferential step: because debt becomes real only when it changes future noticing, workflow should validate trigger-conditioned entries before accepting them.
Workflow consequence: no inquiry debt entry should be accepted without a concrete trigger, decision surface, and accountable noticing mechanism.
Scope: debt registers and future-aware review.
Confidence: high.
