**Revisits / Transformations**
Revisited motifs: the note's earlier anti-objectification pressure, chunk 12's reconstructible judgment path, and chunk 15's warning that nominal openness may still leave the old path of least resistance intact.
Current relation: field-ontological deepening and redistribution critique.
What is preserved: the audit should not confuse its handles with the field itself, and openness should not be mistaken for redistribution.
What is transformed: the problem shifts from units and options to distributions, field-conditioned contestation, reweighting inside judgment paths, and the possibility that openness itself can quietly conserve inherited burden structures.
Grounding basis: current chunk's wording alone.

This chunk begins by testing an apparent escape route. If a unit-centered ontology reifies what the audit wants to track, perhaps `distribution` will do better. The note does not let that substitution pass cheaply. Distribution is useful because it foregrounds apportionment: what counts, what must be justified, what arrives already burdened, what feels ordinary. But the note immediately warns that a larger analytic primitive can harden into another object just as easily. Distribution can become a ledger term that catalogs a moving field rather than a way of staying with patterned relation. This matters because the note is not asking for bigger nouns. It is asking for descriptions that remain sensitive to movement, inheritance, and infrastructural relays.

That shift changes where contestation attaches. If what matters are inherited burden maps, infrastructural defaults, relay patterns, and layered arrangements with no single local author, then critique cannot be satisfied with claim-level attribution alone. The note is clear that refusing a neat addressee may make critique more exact rather than weaker. Contestation may have to pressure a field-condition. That is a demanding move because it rejects the managerial comfort of finding one utterer to hold. A distribution can persist through defaults, relays, and inheritance even where no single actor installed it outright. Critique has to learn how to address that kind of persistence.

The next section then restages the judgment-path problem at a deeper level. Earlier the note asked for reconstructible paths rather than polished conclusions. Here it pushes further: what if what matters most is not the list of alternatives or the conclusion reached, but the movement by which plausibility gradients changed, one feature became weight-bearing, another ceased to govern, and burdens shifted until the later conclusion appeared natural? This is a harder archive problem than uncertainty preservation alone. The note is asking whether reweighting can be curated without flattening it into after-the-fact explanation. That question matters because it treats judgment as a field teaching itself what now matters, not just as an agent selecting among fixed options.

Then the most unsettling turn arrives. Openness, seam-preservation, and refusal of premature closure have been central virtues throughout the note. This section asks whether openness can itself conserve the inherited order. The answer is yes. A tension can remain formally live while the burden distribution stays intact. Nothing is settled, yet the path of least resistance continues to govern. In that case provisionality does not oppose the old order; it postpones the moment when inherited defaults would finally have to justify themselves. This is one of the strongest corrective turns in the whole document because it blocks any easy identification of openness with criticizability.

The test of openness therefore becomes redistributive rather than merely procedural. Has something changed in what counts, what carries, what must answer, what default has become newly burdened? If not, then formal non-finality may be a shelter for the old order rather than a shelter for a better future. That is why this slice sits so importantly between the ontology discussion and the later status-schema discussion. Before the workspace can classify its tensions and branches, it has to ask whether its field-description can capture burdened distributions and whether its celebrated openness is moving anything that matters.

This chunk therefore pushes the note away from static inventory and toward field critique. Units may be too rigid; distributions can become rigid in a larger way. Contestation may need to address defaults and relays rather than speakers alone. Judgment-path reconstruction may have to preserve reweighting rather than option lists. And openness, far from being self-legitimating, has to answer for whether it actually redistributes burden or merely delays the reckoning. If I extend the point, I would say that the slice is trying to shift the audit from an ontology of items to an ontology of apportionment without letting apportionment itself become the new frozen object. That phrasing is mine, but it follows the sequence closely.

# Operational Translation

## Distribution As Patterned Apportionment

Textual pressure: replacing `unit` with `distribution` does not automatically escape reification.
Interpretive translation: distribution is useful only if it names shifting apportionments of burden, salience, ordinariness, and relay rather than a new master object.
Audit-design implication: use distribution language as a field-sensitive handle and test whether it clarifies movement or merely enlarges the ledger.
Scope: audit ontology and descriptive framing.
Confidence: high.

## Field-Conditioned Contestation

Textual pressure: inherited defaults and relay patterns may lack a single neat addressee.
Interpretive translation: answerability can attach to arrangements and field-conditions rather than only statements or utterers.
Audit-design implication: permit contestation of infrastructural defaults, burden maps, and relay structures without forcing critique into person-level attribution simply for manageability.
Scope: contestation registers and critique targets.
Confidence: high.

## Reweighting-Centered Judgment Paths

Textual pressure: option lists and conclusions do not capture the whole judgment path.
Interpretive translation: a strong reconstruction has to preserve changing plausibility gradients, decisive feature shifts, and burden redistributions.
Audit-design implication: reconstruct when features became weight-bearing, when others lost force, and what burden shifts reorganized the scene.
Scope: judgment-path curation and evidence handling.
Confidence: high.

## Conservative Openness Detection

Textual pressure: formal openness can preserve inherited order instead of redistributing it.
Interpretive translation: provisionality becomes conservative when default burdens and path-of-least-resistance advantages remain intact behind non-final language.
Audit-design implication: test openness by what defaults become answerable, what burdens move, and what paths lose pre-licensed status rather than by openness language alone.
Scope: closure review, criticizability assessment, governance analysis.
Confidence: high.

## Redistribution As The Real Test

Textual pressure: the crucial question is not whether the field is open, but whether openness changes what counts and what must answer.
Interpretive translation: criticizability is redistributive before it is rhetorical.
Audit-design implication: assess whether a proposed preservation changes carriers, burdens, or plausibility gradients in practice.
Scope: audit evaluation and field-condition diagnosis.
Confidence: high.

Inferential step: because contestation may need to address arrangements rather than isolated utterers, workflow should support field-level critique directly.

## Reweighting Trace Capture

Inferential step: because judgment paths are partly made of shifting plausibility and burden, workflow should capture those reweightings rather than just options and conclusions.
Workflow consequence: case notes and rereads should record when a feature became decisive, what burden shift changed the field, and how the later conclusion came to seem natural.
Scope: rerun artifacts, judgment reconstruction, handoff materials.
Confidence: high.

Inferential step: because openness can conserve the old order, workflow should test whether a preserved seam actually redistributes anything.
Workflow consequence: before praising an artifact or review state as `open`, ask what default is newly burdened, what path lost pre-licensed ease, and what arrangement now has to answer in a way it did not before.
Scope: audit review, canon maintenance, rerun doctrine.
Confidence: high.
