# Commentary

**Commentary Bounds**
Primary span: `## Continuation: The Decay Of Productive Tension` through `## Practical Consequence For The Audit Workspace`.
Backward / lateral touchpoints: this slice reopens chunk 18's distinction between productive tension and accidental contradiction, then temporalizes it by asking how warranted tensions decay, how they should be retested, and how an audit workspace ought to remember their temporal regime without bureaucratizing them.
Forward reliance: none; this reading stops before `From Units To Distributions`.

**Revisits / Transformations**
Revisited motifs: chunk 18's `productive tension criterion` and the note's earlier worry about locking the audit too tightly to a claim-centered ontology.
Current relation: temporalization, retest design, and local ontological caution.
What is preserved: not every unresolved structure should be flattened, and some preserved tensions really do help judgment.
What is transformed: the problem becomes historical and infrastructural. A tension once worth preserving can decay, its rationale can be lost, and the audit workspace can then mistake inherited surface contradiction for live plurality.
Grounding basis: current chunk's wording alone.

This chunk asks the most dangerous question one can ask about productive tension: what if its warrant expires while its form remains? The note answers without hesitation. A tension decays when the conditions that once made it useful are no longer carried. The horizon split may be forgotten. The plurality that justified the tension may cease to be reconstructible. The carriers that taught successors how to hold it may weaken. The ecology may change so that what was once a necessary preserved seam becomes duplicative conflict. In that case the order inherits contradiction while losing the judgment that once animated it. This is a strong claim because it blocks the pious habit of treating inherited tension as self-justifying depth.

That immediately changes what review has to mean. Periodic rereading alone is not enough. Review becomes ritual when it starts from the presumption that the tension remains warranted, checks only that inherited language still exists, and never reopens the plurality, horizon, or changed ecology that once justified preservation. A real retest has to be able to weaken inherited justification. It reconstructs the original plurality, asks whether that plurality still exists in materially comparable form, tests whether the tension still yields better judgment than reconciliation would, compares current failures and burdens against the tension's supposed value, and allows several outcomes: preserve, reconcile, split, rearticulate, retire. What matters is the willingness to let the inherited rationale lose.

That is why the next section turns to carriers. No single artifact can sustain a real retest. A standing register preserves nameability. Rival rereads admit reinterpretation. Ecology-change triggers register moving conditions. Burden-drift audits expose shifts in who now justifies what. Failure-cluster notes test whether keeping the tension live still pays in practice. Preserved rationale snapshots keep the original grounds reconstructible rather than mythologized. The note's implicit standard is exact: a carrier helps only if it makes visible some combination of original plurality, changed ecology, current burden/failure effects, or a rival articulation. Pure archiving is too weak. Pure presentism is too weak. Formal labels without a judgment-path are too weak.

Temporality then becomes explicit. Continuous monitoring, trigger-based reopening, periodic reread, milestone-gated review: different tensions need different rhythms. This is not just a scheduling problem. It is a recognition that the ecology moves at different speeds and that stale preservation has uneven costs. A fast-moving tension can go rotten under slow review. A stable tension can be dissolved into churn by over-frequent interrogation. Temporal mismatch is itself a failure mode. So the note allows statuses or metadata, but it constrains them tightly. Status is useful when it helps the harness remember what kind of tension this is, what force it carries, what decay risk it faces, what review basis would count, what temporal regime fits. It becomes distortive when it turns into a bureaucratic label that replaces judgment.

The brief practical consequence for the workspace matters more than its small size suggests. After all this work on tensions, regimes, carriers, and statuses, the note pauses to warn the audit not to freeze itself into a simple claim/proposition ontology. Claims remain usable handles, but they are provisional. Future revisions may need commitments, framings, stipulations, vocabularies, exclusions, inheritance relations. This is not an idle addendum. It is the local institutional form of the whole argument just made. If preserved tensions can decay and if statuses can harden into bureaucracy, then the very units of the audit workspace must remain revisable rather than silently metaphysical.

This chunk therefore turns preservation into maintenance of warrant, not maintenance of form. A tension worth keeping once may become stale. Retests have to be able to weaken inherited justification. Multiple carriers are needed because original rationale, changed ecology, burden drift, and rival articulation are different kinds of memory. Temporal regimes have to differ because tensions live under different decay conditions. And the workspace itself has to hold its ontology lightly enough to be revised by what later retests disclose. If I extend the point, I would say that the slice is describing a politics of non-mythic inheritance: preserve tensions only while they still do judgmental work, preserve the reasons as well as the words, and never let the audit's own storage grammar become the latest stale contradiction. That phrasing is mine, but it follows the sequence closely.

# Operational Translation

## Decay Diagnostics For Preserved Tensions

Textual pressure: a tension can remain in place after the conditions that once justified it have disappeared.
Interpretive translation: productive tension decays when the plurality, horizon split, carriers, or ecology that gave it value are no longer carried.
Audit-design implication: treat old preserved tensions as candidates for decay rather than as permanently warranted inheritances.
Scope: tension review and contradiction governance.
Confidence: high.

## Re-Tests That Can Weaken Inheritance

Textual pressure: ritual review merely reauthorizes what already exists.
Interpretive translation: a real retest must be able to reopen and weaken inherited justification.
Audit-design implication: design retests that reconstruct original plurality, assess changed ecology, compare current failures and burdens, and permit preserve/reconcile/split/rearticulate/retire as live outcomes.
Scope: review protocol and preservation governance.
Confidence: high.

## Multi-Carrier Retest Ensembles

Textual pressure: no single artifact can sustain real retesting over time.
Interpretive translation: original rationale, changed conditions, burden drift, failure effects, and rival reinterpretation require different carriers.
Audit-design implication: use ensembles such as tension registers, rationale snapshots, rival rereads, ecology-change triggers, burden-drift audits, and failure-cluster notes.
Scope: retest infrastructure and artifact design.
Confidence: high.

## Temporal-Regime Selection

Textual pressure: different tensions decay at different speeds.
Interpretive translation: continuous monitoring, trigger-based reopening, periodic reread, and milestone-gated review are not interchangeable.
Audit-design implication: select retest rhythm by ecology speed, downstream force, cost of stale preservation, and risk of unnoticed burden drift.
Scope: review cadence and temporal governance.
Confidence: high.

## Minimal Tension Statusing

Textual pressure: the harness needs memory aids, yet over-statusing becomes bureaucracy.
Interpretive translation: statuses are useful only if they guide treatment without replacing rereading.
Audit-design implication: use minimal metadata for tension type, temporal regime, force level, decay risk, and review basis only where those distinctions change carrier ensemble or retest behavior.
Scope: workspace metadata and treatment memory.
Confidence: high.

## Provisional Audit Ontology

Textual pressure: the audit workspace may over-lock itself if it treats claims as the settled unit of analysis.
Interpretive translation: `claim` remains a provisional handle inside a richer possible ontology.
Audit-design implication: keep space for commitments, framings, stipulations, vocabularies, exclusions, and inheritance relations rather than treating proposition-like units as metaphysically final.
Scope: audit ontology and workspace architecture.
Confidence: high.

# Workflow Translation

## Tension Re-Justification Practice

Inferential step: because productive tensions can decay, workflow should periodically ask whether the old rationale still lives rather than merely checking whether the tension still exists.
Workflow consequence: maintenance reviews should reconstruct what plurality was once being preserved, what now pressures it, and whether keeping the tension still yields better judgment than repair or retirement.
Scope: canon maintenance, contradiction review, architecture governance.
Confidence: high.

## Pressure-Based Retest Triggers

Inferential step: because calendar review alone can be ritualized, workflow should reopen tensions when the ecology changes or burdens drift.
Workflow consequence: retest workflows should combine periodic review with trigger events tied to anomaly clusters, burden drift, changed force, or altered practical horizon.
Scope: review scheduling, alerts, governance triggers.
Confidence: high.

## Tension Metadata With Consequences

Inferential step: because statusing helps only when it changes treatment, workflow should couple metadata to concrete review behavior.
Workflow consequence: statuses should alter review cadence, required evidence, and carrier ensemble rather than functioning as descriptive labels only.
Scope: workspace design and audit operations.
Confidence: high.

## Ontology Caveat On Claim-Centric Artifacts

Inferential step: because the workspace risks freezing around one unit vocabulary, workflow should mark claim-heavy artifacts as provisional.
Workflow consequence: specs and audit outputs that lean heavily on `claim` language should record that the underlying ontology remains open to redistribution across framings, commitments, exclusions, vocabularies, and inheritance relations.
Scope: spec writing, canon drafting, workspace doctrine.
Confidence: medium-high.
