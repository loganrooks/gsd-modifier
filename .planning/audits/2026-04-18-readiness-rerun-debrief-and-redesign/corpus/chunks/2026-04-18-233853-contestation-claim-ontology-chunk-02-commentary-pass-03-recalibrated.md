# Commentary

**Commentary Bounds**
Primary span: `## Working Sketch: Two-Layer Audit Ontology` through `### 20. Practical Consequence`.
Backward / lateral touchpoints: this chunk presumes the earlier opening doubt about proposition-centered audit ontology, but its commentary is grounded in this constructive middle slice.
Forward reliance: none; the reading stops at the warrant split and practical consequence.

This chunk is more constructive than the first one, but it keeps interrupting its own construction. The heading already says `Working Sketch`, and the final instruction says to treat the model as a working tool rather than a final metaphysics of discourse. That restraint matters. The note is not announcing that it has found the correct ontology. It is trying to assemble something the audit can use without forgetting why the earlier ontology became suspect.

The proposal is clear: retain a lower layer for explicit, traceable items and add an upper layer for discourse-forming conditions. But the reason for the split matters more than the split itself. `Claim` survives because it is still useful for citation, comparison, review, and burden-of-proof analysis. The note does not repudiate operational tractability. It treats tractability as necessary. The difficulty is that the same tractability can become a quiet reduction. Once discourse is handled through claims, inferences, assumptions, decisions, stipulations, and open questions, whatever pre-shaped their appearance can start to look secondary or non-auditable. The two-layer sketch is an attempt to keep the audit usable without letting usability decide the whole ontology.

The second layer is therefore not just a richer filing cabinet. A framing move, operative distinction, vocabulary regime, exclusion, inheritance relation, sequencing move, closure move, or orientation-setting gesture helps determine what will later count as salient, evidential, realistic, excessive, irrelevant, or decisive. The note is saying that reasonableness is not only something explicit claims possess once stated. It is also distributed in advance by the field in which they arrive. That is why it can say that explicit claims may look individually defensible while underreach, foreclosure, false closure, or inherited narrowing remain active. Layer two does not replace layer one. It conditions it.

Then the note subjects even this repair to suspicion. The richer layer may still objectify. A model introduced to escape proposition-centered flattening can reproduce the same flattening at a higher level if framing, exclusion, orientation, and closure are treated as tidy objects waiting to be catalogued. The note responds by weakening the word `unit`. A unit is to be taken operationally, not metaphysically. The examples matter because they shift the grammar again: not object, but relation; not item, but modulation of a field; not thing, but directional force or constraint on appearance. The note does not throw away handles. It says the audit still needs them. It also says the handles must not pretend to exhaust what they help track.

That is why the later instruction about language matters. Future audit writing may need to alternate between tractable tags and relational, field-sensitive prose. The note is conceding that neither mode is sufficient by itself. Tags without prose turn living relations into inventory. Prose without handles drifts toward impressionism. This is one of the places where the text comes closest to a method, but even here it stops short of doctrine.

The warrant section keeps that alternation disciplined. If the audit moves from explicit claims to field effects, it risks arbitrary projection. The note lists the conditions that make a field-sensitive reading stronger: recurrence across artifacts, asymmetry between what is named and what remains unarticulated, stable vocabulary, sequencing effects, downstream consequence patterns, contrast with plausible omitted alternatives, and rival readings that have been comparatively weakened. The practical consequence that follows is exact: separate direct explicit claims, trace patterns that suggest field effects, and stronger or weaker interpretations of those patterns. My own summary risks simplifying the point if I say that the note wants a layered hermeneutics. The text is narrower and more demanding than that. It wants an audit that can register conditioning relations without pretending either that they are self-evident objects or that they are beyond disciplined argument. The sketch remains working, which is another way of saying the problem has been given a more usable form without being closed.

# Operational Translation

1. Textual pressure: the two-layer model is introduced as a pragmatic compromise and explicitly marked as a working tool rather than final metaphysics. Interpretive translation: the schema should support audit work without claiming exhaustive ontological authority. Audit-design implication: define the ontology as provisional and revisable, with explicit room for later adjustment if the layers start obscuring what they were meant to reveal. Scope: ontology charter and revision rules. Confidence: high.

2. Textual pressure: operational units remain valuable for traceability, citation, comparison, and burden-of-proof analysis. Interpretive translation: explicit-assertion tracking is still indispensable. Audit-design implication: keep a stable operational layer for claims, inferences, assumptions, decisions, stipulations, and open questions rather than dissolving everything into contextual prose. Scope: item extraction and review structure. Confidence: high.

3. Textual pressure: discourse-forming units determine what counts as salient, evidential, realistic, excessive, irrelevant, or decisive. Interpretive translation: framing and exclusion are conditions on what can later appear as reasonable judgment, not peripheral annotations. Audit-design implication: record discourse-forming readings separately and link them to the option-space, evidence standards, or burden distributions they shape. Scope: framing analysis, warrant analysis, contestation registers. Confidence: high.

4. Textual pressure: the richer layer can still objectify if its units are treated as tidy catalogued things. Interpretive translation: tags should function as provisional handles rather than exhaustive captures. Audit-design implication: require each discourse-forming tag to be accompanied by short relational prose explaining how it operates in context and what it conditions. Scope: annotation format and reviewer guidance. Confidence: medium-high.

5. Textual pressure: field-sensitive readings require convergent traces and should not be presented as self-evident. Interpretive translation: the audit must distinguish observation, pattern, and interpretation, and grade the strength of the last step. Audit-design implication: report in three lanes, direct explicit claim, trace pattern, and interpretive conclusion with stated strength and visible grounds. Scope: evidence handling, reporting, contestation protocol. Confidence: high.

# Workflow Translation

1. Inferential step: because the note keeps operational units while adding discourse-forming ones, workflow should separate extraction from field-reading without collapsing either. Workflow consequence: use a first pass for explicit traceable items and a second pass for framing, exclusion, inheritance, sequencing, orientation, and closure effects. Scope: audit procedure, review sequence, postmortem analysis. Confidence: high.

2. Inferential step: because the richer layer can itself re-objectify what it studies, workflow needs a check against overconfident tagging. Workflow consequence: require reviewers to state whether a discourse-forming designation is functioning as a minimal handle or as a stronger relational claim, and require a short prose explanation rather than a bare label. Scope: review templates, prompt design, handoff artifacts. Confidence: medium-high.

3. Inferential step: because warrant depends on convergent traces and on a visible separation between trace and interpretation, workflow must make grounds auditable one inferential layer at a time. Workflow consequence: audit outputs should separate direct citation, trace pattern, and interpretive conclusion, and should cite the recurrence, asymmetry, sequencing effect, omitted alternative, or rival-reading comparison that supports the move between them. Scope: audit outputs, decision-log critique, canon-update review. Confidence: high.
