# Wave-1 Spec: Outcome And Underreach Audit

Status: draft lane spec  
Date: 2026-04-19

## Lane

- wave: `1`
- lane: `outcome-and-underreach-audit`
- packet: [02-outcome-and-underreach-audit-packet.md](../packets/02-outcome-and-underreach-audit-packet.md)

## Purpose

- [g:r:i] Judge what the readiness package genuinely achieved, what was mostly procedural churn or local optimization, and where it translated earned doctrine back down into tame closure.
- [g:r:i] Distinguish weak mapping from weak judgment instead of flattening all underreach into one generic miss category.

## Primary Questions

1. What outcomes were real and load-bearing?
2. What outcomes were mostly procedural churn, review recycling, or local optimization?
3. Where did doctrine get translated back down into `safe enough`, `patch lightly`, or other tame forms?
4. Where did the package underreach because it optimized for limiting disruption rather than maximizing attainable improvement?
5. How much of the underreach came from weak mapping versus weak judgment?

## Required Posture

- [g:r:i] Do not let a few real gains become a blanket acquittal.
- [g:r:i] Do not let a few misses erase the real doctrine and mapping work that did land.
- [g:r:i] If the lane trends toward `mostly fine`, `directionally right`, or `only local undercarry`, open the packet's challenge stage before finalizing any judgment.

## Required Method

1. Read the packet in staged order.
2. Build an outcome register first.
3. Build an underreach register second.
4. Then classify underreach sources into at least:
   - weak mapping
   - weak judgment
   - interaction effects between the two
5. Make the anti-tame quota concrete by naming stronger interventions considered and rejected.

## Required Output

Produce one markdown artifact under `wave-1/outputs/` with these sections:

1. `Overall Outcome And Underreach Judgment`
2. `Outcome Register`
3. `Underreach Register`
4. `Mapping Versus Judgment Attribution`
5. `Anchor Cases`
6. `Non-Intervention Ledger`
7. `Interventions Considered And Rejected`
8. `What Later Lanes Must Pressure`

## Required Registers

- `inheritance-disposition matrix`
- `suppressed-opportunity register`
- `non-intervention ledger`
- `interventions considered and rejected ledger`

## Failure Conditions

- [g:r:i] Fails if it treats procedural movement as substantive gain without testing effect.
- [g:r:i] Fails if it uses `undercarry` language without naming what stronger move was left on the table.
- [g:r:i] Fails if it cannot name at least `3` stronger interventions it considered and rejected.
