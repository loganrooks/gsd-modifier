# Wave-1 Spec: Mission Reconstruction

Status: draft lane spec  
Date: 2026-04-19

## Lane

- wave: `1`
- lane: `mission-reconstruction`
- packet: [01-mission-reconstruction-packet.md](../packets/01-mission-reconstruction-packet.md)

## Purpose

- [g:r:i] Reconstruct what the readiness package was actually trying to do at the levels of governance, harness behavior, review posture, and future-preserving planning.
- [g:r:i] Distinguish explicit mission statements from implicit but load-bearing mission commitments.
- [g:r:i] Produce a reusable mission frame for the other Wave-1 lanes without pretending this lane is sovereign.

## Primary Questions

1. What explicit mission did the readiness package state?
2. What implicit mission commitments were still load-bearing in sequencing, doctrine, and adjudication?
3. What theory of change did the package seem to assume about governance, harness behavior, and future-preserving workflow?
4. Where was the mission internally coherent, and where did it carry unresolved tension or contradiction?
5. Which parts of that mission deserve preservation, reinterpretation, supersession, or explicit dropping?

## Required Posture

- [g:r:i] Do not collapse mission reconstruction into later outcome judgment.
- [g:r:i] Do not treat the bridge audit's guarded-hybrid verdict as the final mission statement.
- [g:r:i] Do not treat the session framing brief as unquestionable premise.
- [g:r:i] If the lane starts treating the mission as settled too quickly, open the packet's challenge stage and revise the provisional mission explicitly.

## Required Method

1. Read the packet in staged order.
2. Produce a provisional mission sketch after Stage 1.
3. Separate:
   - explicit mission statements
   - implicit but load-bearing mission commitments
   - tensions or contradictions between the two
4. If Stage 2 is opened, add a `Mission Correction Note` rather than silently smoothing away pressure.
5. End with a starter inheritance-disposition matrix that later lanes can refine rather than merely repeat.

## Required Output

Produce one markdown artifact under `wave-1/outputs/` with these sections:

1. `Overall Mission Judgment`
2. `Explicit Mission`
3. `Implicit Load-Bearing Mission`
4. `Theory Of Change`
5. `Mission Tensions And Contradictions`
6. `Starter Inheritance-Disposition Matrix`
7. `Interventions Considered And Rejected`
8. `Open Questions For Later Lanes`

## Required Registers

- `theory-of-change summary`
- `inheritance-disposition matrix`
- `element-granular inheritance-disposition matrix for top load-bearing artifacts or doctrines`
- `interventions considered and rejected ledger`

## Failure Conditions

- [g:r:i] Fails if it merely paraphrases the plan or doctrine without reconstructing implicit mission.
- [g:r:i] Fails if it treats mission coherence as proof of package adequacy.
- [g:r:i] Fails if it cannot name at least `3` stronger interventions it considered and rejected for this lane, unless it explicitly justifies why that quota is inapplicable.
