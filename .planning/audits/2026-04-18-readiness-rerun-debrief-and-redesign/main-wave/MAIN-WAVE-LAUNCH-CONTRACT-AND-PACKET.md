- [g:c+i] Non-intervention is not a neutral default; any `leave it alone`, `keep it local`, or `no new lane` recommendation must answer the anti-regret burden. - [g:c+i] Compare lanes rather than flattening them into consensus or prestige deference. Sources: .planning/audits/2026-04-18-readiness-rerun-debrief-and-redesign/governance/AUDIT-CHARTER.md:15-16.
- [g:r:i] Preserve future-flexibility statuses explicitly: `direct doctrine`, `bounded-open branches`, `preserve-only seams`, `reversal-sensitive boundaries`, and `inquiry debt`.
- [g:r:i] Keep carriage improvement distinct from doctrine promotion. A lane may earn a local proposal or reviewer-register carry without yet earning canon rewrite.
- [g:c+i] Wave-1 spec-writing must carry forward the stronger anti-tame and burden-of-proof rules already earned in the wider setup packet rather than silently thinning them back down at handoff. ## Main-Wave Architecture

### Wave 1: Parallel Analytic Lanes

- [g:r:i] Wave 1 launches in parallel. Non-`mission-reconstruction` lanes may restate provisional mission assumptions where needed, but must mark them as provisional and compare them against the mission lane during disposition or reread rather than treating them as settled upstream truth.
- [g:r:i] If `mapping-adequacy-and-comparative-mapping` cannot stay inside packet bounds without flattening distinct defect types, the lane spec must split adequacy and comparative mapping into explicit internal subsections or record a pre-launch escalation note rather than silently compressing them. Sources: .planning/audits/2026-04-18-readiness-rerun-debrief-and-redesign/governance/EVIDENCE-ARCHITECTURE.md:102-107, .planning/audits/2026-04-18-readiness-rerun-debrief-and-redesign/lane-reviews/lane-05-opus47-max-main-wave-contract-cross-review.md:32-33, .planning/audits/2026-04-18-readiness-rerun-debrief-and-redesign/lane-reviews/lane-05-gpt54-xhigh-main-wave-contract-cross-review.md:21-25.

1. `mission-reconstruction`
   - question: what was the readiness package actually trying to do at the level of governance, harness behavior, review posture, and future-preserving planning?
   - primary output: theory-of-change summary plus inheritance-disposition starter matrix

2. `outcome-and-underreach-audit`
   - question: what did the package genuinely achieve, where did it translate doctrine back down into tame closure, and where did it underreach?
   - primary output: outcome register, underreach register, first anchor-case analysis

3. `mapping-adequacy-and-comparative-mapping`
   - question: how do the original readiness map, the bridge audit, the revised docs terrain, and runtime / harness behavior compare, and what still separates current mapping from intervention-ready mapping?
   - primary output: mapping-confidence register, mismatch matrix, runtime-vs-map divergence notes

4. `operator-orchestration-pressure`
   - question: what operator-bandwidth, sequencing, or orchestration pressures contributed to underreach independently of mapping weakness?
   - primary output: operator-pressure register and alternative explanation notes

### Wave 2: Opportunity And Rerun-Shape Lanes

5. `suppressed-opportunity-and-non-intervention`
   - consumes Wave 1 outputs
   - question: which stronger interventions were prematurely excluded, narrowed, softened, or deferred, and what regret analysis would justify rejecting them now?
   - primary output: suppressed-opportunity register plus interventions-considered-and-rejected ledger

6. `rerun-design`
   - consumes Wave 1 outputs plus lane 5
   - question: what second-attempt program shape is now justified, under what execution-capacity model, and with what branch/switch triggers?
   - primary output: rerun-design options, prerequisites list, recommended next-program options

### Wave 3: Synthesis

7. `synthesis`
   - consumes prior lane outputs rather than the whole raw corpus by default
   - question: what does the workspace now recommend as the next honest program, what remains contested, and what should stay open?
   - primary output: recommended next program and explicit unresolved register

- [g:r:i] Do not dump the full raw corpus into one prestige-model lane. Packet by question.
- [g:r:i] Do not let the bridge audit become either forgotten history or unquestioned floor.
