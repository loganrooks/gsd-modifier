# Entry Surface / Project Uplift Bundle Cross-Vendor Spec

## Framing

- [g:r:i] Audit the local bundle `36 + 37 + 38 + 39` as one bounded design family.
- [g:r:i] The governing task is not to treat the bundle as an admission-bar object or a closure question.
- [g:r:i] The governing task is to determine how this bundle should be inherited:
  - carried substantially as written
  - revised
  - split
  - resequenced
  - or widened where important concern families or carrier placements still thin
- [g:r:i] Keep the review out of threshold language, deficit-only framing, and binary recommendation shapes.

## Primary Questions

1. What in the bundle already carries the family strongly?
2. Where does the bundle still thin, blur, or compress distinct jobs that should stay separate?
3. Which concerns are still weakly placed, or could surface in stronger carriers than the bundle currently uses?
4. Which parts of `39` are the right composition-layer ownership, and which parts still lean too far toward absorbing specialist-owner work?
5. What stronger first implementation slice becomes visible once the full bundle is read together?
6. What should remain explicit later-family work rather than being packed into the first slice?
7. What review or verification shape would most clearly reveal whether the first slice increases entry/uplift carry in practice?

## Required Output Shape

Use these exact section headings:

1. `What The Bundle Already Carries Strongly`
2. `Where The Bundle Still Thins Or Compresses Distinct Jobs`
3. `Concerns That Could Surface In Stronger Carriers`
4. `What The Workflow Should Own`
5. `What Should Stay With Specialist Surfaces`
6. `Best First Implementation Slice`

8. `How To Verify Stronger Entry/Uplift Carry`
9. `How This Bundle Should Be Inherited`

Inside section `9`, separate:
- `Carry Forward`
- `Revise Before Carry`
- `Hold For Later`
