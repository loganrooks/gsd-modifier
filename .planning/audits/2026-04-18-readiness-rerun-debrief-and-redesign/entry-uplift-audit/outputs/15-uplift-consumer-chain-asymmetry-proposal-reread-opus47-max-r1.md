## How The Classification Frame Should Travel

- [e:c+r:i] The three-valued frame is well-matched to the live consumer chain:
  - `deepen in place` carriers are those with an already-shaped slot that can receive an additional held-runtime line without a shared reference. Candidates with the most direct fit include `transition.md` with `templates/state.md` (the state-continuity template already carries a compact `Future Carry Forward` digest structurally parallel to the scalar summary that `116` now writes), `new-milestone.md` together with `complete-milestone.md` (already read `LONG-ARC.md` plus `Future Carry Forward` at both boundaries), and `health.md` (already routes later uplift explicitly and pairs repair with structural validation).
  - `attach through a shared reference` carriers are those whose read-packet doctrine already points to one shared surface. The `mandatory-initial-read.md` reference from `25` is the natural attach surface, and its consumers from `25`/`26`/`27`/`28` would inherit held-runtime posture through one reference edit rather than through a per-consumer growth. This classification would carry the most consumers at the lowest prose cost.
  - `explicitly held` carriers are those where any carry would drag in either route-translation appetite or typed-carrier pressure. The strongest candidates are the verifier agent contract and the verifier lifecycle surfaces, since held-runtime awareness at phase close slides into "what does compatibility mean under future-preservation review" — a real question the compatibility family is not currently answering. Discuss/plan/execute entry points also sit here for as long as family-6 has not disclosed whether their asymmetry is a registry question, a translation question, or a doctrine question.
- [d:r:i] The frame is complete in count but thin along one axis worth intensifying inside `118` before implementation: the distinction between carriers that **surface read-only** (what the four already-landed surfaces do) and carriers that **recommend writes** (what `$gsd-uplift-project` currently uniquely does). `deepen in place` on the read-only side is a prose-level addition. `deepen in place` on the write-recommending side widens `compatibility_drift_reasons` or its equivalent. The two are not the same decision. Naming the axis inside the Required Classification section keeps the per-carrier classification honest when the implementation slice arrives.
- [d:r:i] The frame also leaves the **test-frontier question** unspoken. `40`'s focused route-contract test, plus the per-slice contract tests in `19-29`, establish a register in which any consumer-side held-runtime awareness would have to decide whether its test frontier widens in parallel. That is classification-adjacent rather than classification-internal; a short note inside `118` that test widening is scoped with each carrier rather than handled globally would keep the implementation slice from backing into either a sweeping test retrofit or a silent test gap.
