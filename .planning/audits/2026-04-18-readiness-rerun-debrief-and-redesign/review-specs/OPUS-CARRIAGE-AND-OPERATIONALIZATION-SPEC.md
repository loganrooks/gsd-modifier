## Lane Question

Given the current audit workspace, selected repo-local carrier surfaces, and a bounded commentary-corpus packet, which corpus distinctions improve carriage quality enough to justify:

1. a local proposal now
2. a bounded next stress test
3. reviewer-register use only
4. or no inheritance on this evidence

Subquestions:

1. Where do the current surfaces already carry the work adequately?
2. Where is carriage thin, brittle, overly operator-dependent, or closure-prone?
3. Which corpus distinctions add real operational leverage rather than only sharper labels?
4. Which target surfaces are the best candidates for bounded operationalization?
5. What should explicitly not be rewritten yet, and why?

## Carriage Criteria

Use these criteria explicitly. A candidate gain is stronger when it improves several at once.

1. diagnostic precision
2. burden / authority visibility
3. intervention routing
4. reproducibility by later reviewers
5. reduced dependence on unusually strong operator prose or heroics
6. indirect-carrier legibility
7. openness that actually redistributes defaults rather than only defers
8. technical or procedural implementability
9. improved attainable-outcome quality rather than merely adequate pass/fail sufficiency

## Method

### Stage 0: Baseline Surface Survey

After Stage 0 and Stage 1 reading, but before Stage 2:

1. write a `Baseline Surface Survey`
2. for each surface A-D, state:
   - what the current surface already carries well
   - where carriage seems thin, brittle, ceremonial, or overly prose-dependent
   - what current non-change or defer positions seem strongest
   - what remains unclear without further pressure

### Stage 1: Challenge-And-Corpus Reread

After Stage 2 reading:

1. reread each surface through only the corpus distinctions and challenge artifacts that actually matter
2. do not force every distinction into every surface
3. for each surface, state:
   - which distinctions or challenge pressures changed your reading
   - whether the gain is diagnostic, procedural, structural, or only rhetorical
   - what concrete candidate operationalizations now appear

### Stage 2: Operationalization Judgment

For each candidate operationalization, classify it as exactly one primary disposition:

- `ready_for_local_proposal`
- `ready_for_bounded_stress_test`
- `reviewer_register_only`
- `not_earned_on_this_evidence`

Then defend the classification.

### Stage 3: Sequence Recommendation

Produce an ordered local sequence that answers:

1. what, if anything, should be proposed locally now
2. what should be tested next before any proposal
3. what should remain reviewer-register only
4. what should explicitly not be rewritten yet

### Stage 4: Rejected-Stronger-Moves Register

Across the lane as a whole, identify at least `3` stronger candidate operationalizations you considered and did not classify as `ready_for_local_proposal`.

For each one, state:

1. why it was a plausible stronger move
2. what gain it might have delivered
3. why it was downgraded to `ready_for_bounded_stress_test`, `reviewer_register_only`, or `not_earned_on_this_evidence`
4. what evidence would justify reopening it

## Anti-Misread Rules

- [g:r:i] If a corpus distinction preserves the same recommendation but materially improves how burden, authority, indirect carriers, or openness are carried, that can still count as more than `reviewer_register_only`.
- [g:r:i] If a distinction only supplies a sharper label without changing diagnosis, routing, reproducibility, or implementability, classify it as `reviewer_register_only`, not as operational gain.
- [g:r:i] Do not use lane-03 as a global `no-change` verdict. Its authority is narrower than this lane's question.
- [g:r:i] Do not punish the current baseline unfairly by pretending existing doctrine or workflow prose is weaker than it is.
- [g:r:i] Do not reward the corpus unfairly just because it makes the prose sound more philosophical or more compressed.
- [g:r:i] Do not let `baseline already carries this` win by default if the corpus makes a materially better attainable outcome, more reproducible review, or more operationally legible carrier design visible.
- [g:r:i] Do not force `CLAIM-TYPES.md` to be the flagship target merely because it is symbolically attractive.
- [g:r:i] Do not widen into ecosystem doctrine, upstream GSD proposals, or repo-wide template rewrites.

## Burden-Of-Proof Rules

- [g:r:i] Any recommendation for `not_earned_on_this_evidence` or `reviewer_register_only` must say what gain was considered and rejected, what evidence would reopen it, and why non-change is not just ease masquerading as caution.
- [g:r:i] Any recommendation for `ready_for_local_proposal` must say what closure pressure it introduces, what narrower move was considered, and why the stronger move is still justified.
- [g:r:i] Any recommendation for `ready_for_bounded_stress_test` must name the minimum discriminating test rather than asking for another vague research round.
- [g:r:i] A merely adequate current surface is not enough reason to reject change. If the baseline is judged sufficient, the lane must still say whether a materially stronger, more legible, or less heroics-dependent carriage was available and why it was not yet justified.
