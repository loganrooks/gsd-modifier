# Harness Parallelization Field Map And Diagnosis Audit Spec

## Task

- [g:r:i] Diagnose the current parallelization field across three domains:
  - vanilla GSD in action
  - the modified harness in action
  - the harness-improvement program itself
- [g:r:i] The lane should map the field first and only then judge what later doctrine, workflow, agent, or governance changes are earned.

## Required Judgment Shape

- [g:r:i] Keep the three domains explicit throughout. Do not let them blur into one flattened `parallelization` answer.
- [g:r:i] Distinguish:
  - explicit governed parallelization
  - implied but undernamed or under-governed parallelization
  - serial posture that appears principled
  - serial posture that appears more accidental or historically inherited
- [g:r:i] Keep the difference between declared capability and governed operator contract explicit.
- [g:r:i] Keep the difference between:
  - safe earned overlap
  - promising but not-yet-governed overlap
  - overlap that would likely degrade coherence, continuity, or software quality
- [g:r:i] If the strongest representation is not one universal rule but one map of classes, boundaries, and prerequisites, say so directly.

## Avoid

- [g:r:i] Do not answer only with a wishlist of new parallelization opportunities.
- [g:r:i] Do not answer only with `there is already wave-based execution` or `there are already spawned agents`.
- [g:r:i] Do not let background-agent wording, a `Task()` mapping note, or a capability matrix stand in for actual workflow/operator governance.
- [g:r:i] Do not conflate faster throughput with better harness quality.
- [g:r:i] Do not widen into generic portability, telemetry, or deployment-program design.
- [g:r:i] Do not reopen host-product planning horizons as if they were the harness-program far horizon for this lane.
