  - direct rerun scrutiny of how the narrow gate became the headline
  - stronger corrective uplift rather than a light carry-forward repair

## What The Trial Shows

- [d:r:i] The vocabulary changed response family across the pair.
- [d:r:i] `Explicit miss` supported a harder backward indictment and broader correction path.
- [d:r:i] `Traceable undercarry` supported a narrower repair: keep the current default, but tighten how the alternative space is carried and judged.
- [d:r:i] That difference is the point. If both cases had ended in the same remedy family, the vocabulary would have looked ornamental.

## What The Trial Does Not Show

- [d:r:i] It does not justify immediate codification of the full vocabulary into `../planning-notes/QUESTION-SET.md`, `CLAIM-TYPES.md`, or standing audit doctrine.
- [d:r:i] It does not prove the labels are complete, stable, or exhaustive.
- [d:r:i] It does not yet test the weakest category, `retrospective possibility`, on a concrete case.
- [d:r:i] It does not license a hidden seriousness ladder. The trial only earns a guide-shaped local use: label plus response family, without score, total, or composite severity.

## Disposition

- [d:r:i] Keep the graded-underreach vocabulary live at reviewer-register level inside this audit setup.
- [d:r:i] Treat the current yield as real but provisional: the labels altered remedy style on this pair, so they earned more than decorative phrasing.
- [d:r:i] Do not yet patch standing doctrine or required templates. First use the vocabulary in the next proposal-disposition / launch-readiness reread and check whether it continues to change treatment rather than only prose.

## Anti-Scoring Guard

- [g:r:i] Read this trial as a response-calibration exercise, not as a seriousness scale.
- [g:r:i] `Explicit miss` does not automatically mean maximal redesign, and `traceable undercarry` does not automatically mean mild concern. Consequence profile still matters separately from continuity strength.
- [g:r:i] If later use collapses the labels into a covert severity ladder, this trial should be treated as misapplied rather than as proof that the vocabulary itself was a mistake.
