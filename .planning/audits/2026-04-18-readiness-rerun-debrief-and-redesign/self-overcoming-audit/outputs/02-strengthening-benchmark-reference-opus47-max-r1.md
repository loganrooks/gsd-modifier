## Where The Current Pair Overcompresses Distinct Forms

- [d:r:i] The two carry routes flatten a third distinction the entries themselves expose. Entry 1 was the *opening move* that created a new carrier on an existing surface. Entry 2 was an *adjacent alignment* across research, planner, checker, and agent-contract surfaces that already carried related shape. Both route as `current-phase intensification`, but "land first new bucket" and "propagate across adjacent contracts" are different moves with different verification burdens. The reference's Route Decision Rule — "consumer surface already exists + bounded enough for current batch + effect auditable now" — reads identically for both and offers no pressure to distinguish. Sources: [intervention-proposals/34-strengthening-opportunity-benchmark-packet.md](../../intervention-proposals/34-strengthening-opportunity-benchmark-packet.md:31), [intervention-proposals/34-strengthening-opportunity-benchmark-packet.md](../../intervention-proposals/34-strengthening-opportunity-benchmark-packet.md:49), [intervention-proposals/35-strengthening-opportunity-reference-surface.md](../../intervention-proposals/35-strengthening-opportunity-reference-surface.md:33).
- [d:r:i] "Current strength" is used as a single bucket but the four entries carry it from four structurally different origins:
  - entry 1 — prior audit diagnosis plus companion-layer proposal chain gave the carrier theoretical authority *before* the slice landed
  - entry 2 — existing skill/method/output surfaces with their own internal shape
  - entries 3 and 4 — a diagnostic register (proposal `29`) identifying a surface that already carries something tangential, not a landed carrier of the family itself

  These are not the same kind of "already carrying something real". A later reader could glue them into one "is there *any* existing surface we can point to?" admission test, which is a gate shape the pair is trying to avoid. Sources: [intervention-proposals/34-strengthening-opportunity-benchmark-packet.md](../../intervention-proposals/34-strengthening-opportunity-benchmark-packet.md:27), [intervention-proposals/34-strengthening-opportunity-benchmark-packet.md](../../intervention-proposals/34-strengthening-opportunity-benchmark-packet.md:45), [intervention-proposals/34-strengthening-opportunity-benchmark-packet.md](../../intervention-proposals/34-strengthening-opportunity-benchmark-packet.md:63), [intervention-proposals/34-strengthening-opportunity-benchmark-packet.md](../../intervention-proposals/34-strengthening-opportunity-benchmark-packet.md:81).
- [d:r:i] Every optionality effect currently logged is *preservation-type*: it stops later collapse, keeps pressure alive, or makes something auditable that would otherwise dissolve. None of the four entries demonstrates an *opening-type* effect — a move that actively enables a later action that was not previously available. Both are valid, but the reference currently does not require the candidate to say which kind it is, so preservation-type framing can crowd out opening-type without anyone noticing.
