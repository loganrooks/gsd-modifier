## Current Strengths

- [e:c+r:i] The chain has a real steering contract, not just a pre-planning questionnaire. `discuss-phase` is explicitly told to capture assumptions, open questions, epistemic guardrails, canonical refs, code context, future awareness, and deferred ideas; it also separates resumable checkpoint state, canonical steering, and audit log artifacts instead of flattening them into one file. That aligns well with the repo's artifact-governance and planning-quality doctrine. Sources: `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/discuss-phase.md:461-501,891-927,939-1108`; `.codex/get-shit-done/templates/context.md:3-12,57-110,126-152`; `.planning/AGENTS.md:35-40,42-58,84-94`; `ARTIFACT-GOVERNANCE.md:7-41,66-85`.
- [e:c+r:i] Exploratory planning is not allowed to pretend context is optional. `plan-phase` hard-stops when exploratory mode lacks `CONTEXT.md` unless the operator explicitly uses `--allow-no-context`, and it states the exact guarantees that are being weakened. That is a strong protocol-level defense against context-free planning theater. Sources: `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/plan-phase.md:223-252`; `AGENTS.md:41-45`; `.planning/AGENTS.md:114-120`.
- [e:c+r:i] The plan contract is unusually good at fighting shallow execution. `PLAN.md` requires `future_preservation`, `must_haves`, concrete `read_first`, exact `action`, and grep-verifiable `acceptance_criteria`; `plan-phase` explains why those fields matter; `gsd-plan-checker` separately blocks missing requirement coverage, missing `VALIDATION.md`, and silent scope reduction. This is not merely decorative structure. Sources: `.codex/get-shit-done/templates/phase-prompt.md:14-34,64-125,132-179`; `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/plan-phase.md:694-757,828-845`; `.codex/agents/gsd-plan-checker.md:100-126,334-390,624-688`.
- [e:c+r:i] The execution tail includes real non-artifact checks. `execute-phase` guards parallelism via `files_modified` overlap, preserves single-writer ownership of shared planning docs under worktrees, runs a substantive code-review workflow, runs prior-phase regression tests, blocks on schema drift when verification would otherwise be a false positive, and then invokes goal-backward verification. Those are meaningful excellence surfaces, not just completion banners. Sources: `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/execute-phase.md:247-283,316-366,551-565,744-775,827-949,954-999`; `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/code-review.md:1-3,73-87,119-150,200-233`.
- [e:c+r:i] The verification/UAT tail is stronger than a typical "done means merged" flow. `gsd-verifier` explicitly distrusts summaries, merges roadmap success criteria with plan must-haves, checks orphaned requirements, and forces `human_needed` when human judgment remains. `verify-work` then turns `SUMMARY.md` into structured user-observable tests and even injects a cold-start smoke test for startup-sensitive changes. Sources: `.codex/agents/gsd-verifier.md:18-30,135-152,375-391,495-540,589-626`; `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/verify-work.md:137-168,171-240`; `WORKFLOW.md:94-109`.

## Where The Workflow Chain Rewards Excellence

- [e:c+r:i] It rewards steering rigor by insisting that ambiguity become a named artifact class instead of ambient chat residue. `working_model`, `open_questions`, `epistemic_guardrails`, `canonical_refs`, and the four-bucket `future_awareness` contract all create places where uncertainty can remain visible without being silently widened into scope. Sources: `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/discuss-phase.md:478-486,792-803,956-1108`; `.codex/get-shit-done/templates/context.md:48-55,66-82,93-143`; `.planning/AGENTS.md:42-58,84-94`.
- [e:c+r:i] It rewards future-aware planning rather than present-only task decomposition. `plan-phase` tells the planner to map every material future-awareness item to a preserved seam, sequencing choice, validation task, or explicit non-action rationale, and the checker can fail generic or missing `future_preservation`. That is a real anti-flattening mechanism. Sources: `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/plan-phase.md:694-703,710-715,840-845`; `.codex/get-shit-done/templates/phase-prompt.md:25-34,141-149,162-165`; `.planning/readiness/phase-01-rerun/AUDITS/checkpoint-3-gsd-workflow-chain-and-artifact-contracts.md:55-60`.
- [e:c+r:i] It rewards concrete executable thought over vague "implement X" plans. The planner is explicitly told that the executor should be able to work from the plan text alone, and the checker separately validates structure, specificity, dependency graph, key links, and must-have derivation. That is strong protocol pressure against fake precision. Sources: `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/plan-phase.md:718-757`; `.codex/agents/gsd-plan-checker.md:624-688`; `.codex/get-shit-done/templates/phase-prompt.md:160-179`.
- [e:c+r:i] It rewards honest downstream verification. The verifier's decision tree prevents `passed` when human verification exists, checks requirements against actual implementation evidence, and conservatively filters only clearly later-phase work into `deferred`. That is an excellence-oriented verification posture, not merely a summary confirmation loop. Sources: `.codex/agents/gsd-verifier.md:375-391,495-540,589-621`; `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/execute-phase.md:954-1058`.
- [e:c+r:i] It rewards continuity and auditability. `DISCUSS-CHECKPOINT.json`, `DISCUSSION-LOG.md`, `SUMMARY.md`, `VERIFICATION.md`, `HUMAN-UAT.md`, and `UAT.md` are all designed to survive session loss or later reread instead of disappearing into chat memory. That fits the repo's continuity and artifact-governance expectations well. Sources: `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/discuss-phase.md:891-927,941-943`; `.codex/get-shit-done/templates/summary.md:132-148`; `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/execute-phase.md:1000-1058`; `WORKFLOW.md:111-117`; `ARTIFACT-GOVERNANCE.md:66-85`.

## Where The Workflow Chain Settles For Pass/Fail Closure

- [d:c+r:i] Research is integrated, but research quality is under-gated relative to planning and verification quality. The researcher can be `complete` or `blocked`, Nyquist can be skipped when research is absent, and there is no direct "research adequacy" or "open-questions disposition" gate before planning. That means the chain strongly pressures plan quality while only lightly pressuring research quality. Sources: `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/research-phase.md:1-5,34-57`; `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/plan-phase.md:361-414,420-444`; `.planning/readiness/phase-01-rerun/AUDITS/checkpoint-4-gsd-workflow-chain-and-artifact-contracts-excellence-spec.md:47-52`.
- [d:c+r:i] Several important planning gates still end in cheap closure options. After stalled revisions the operator can `Proceed anyway`; after uncovered requirements/context features the operator can move them to the next phase or proceed anyway. Those exits may be useful generically, but in this repo they are exactly the kind of "good enough to pass" paths the readiness plan warns against. Sources: `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/plan-phase.md:902-918,963-1010`; `AGENTS.md:49-69`; `.planning/AGENTS.md:100-107`; `.planning/readiness/phase-01-rerun/PLAN.md:132-145`.
- [d:c+r:i] Auto mode materially thins excellence pressure. The checkpoint contract says auto mode auto-approves human-verify checkpoints and auto-selects the first decision option; the discuss workflow likewise auto-selects grounded defaults and then enforces a single-pass cap. That is a reasonable throughput mode, but it is not an excellence mode for doctrine-sensitive reruns. Sources: `.codex/get-shit-done/references/checkpoints.md:4-12,16-37`; `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/discuss-phase.md:790-810`; `.planning/readiness/phase-01-rerun/AUDITS/checkpoint-4-workflow-harness-excellence-launch-spec.md:25-38,112-119`.
- [d:c+r:i] Wave completion still leans on artifact presence more than substantive proof. `execute-phase` intentionally treats `SUMMARY.md` existence, recent commits, and the absence of `Self-Check: FAILED` as practical completion evidence; that is resilient, but it also means the strongest execution-time completion signal is still "artifact exists plus no obvious red flag." Sources: `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/execute-phase.md:446-460,567-595`; `.codex/get-shit-done/references/agent-contracts.md:56-79`.
- [d:c+r:i] Code review is real but deliberately non-blocking. `execute-phase` requires a code-review step, yet review issues and even review-tool failure never block the flow. Given the repo's stated preference for separate verification/review before merge, this is a conscious closure-biased choice rather than an absence of review machinery. Sources: `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/execute-phase.md:744-775`; `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/code-review.md:1-3,73-87`; `WORKFLOW.md:94-99`.
- [d:c+r:i] Human verification debt can coexist with nominal phase completion. `execute-phase` persists `HUMAN-UAT.md`, presents the human-required items, and then lets `"approved"` proceed to `update_roadmap` while the UAT artifact remains `partial`. That preserves the debt, but it also makes "complete enough to advance" easier than "cleanly closed." Sources: `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/execute-phase.md:1000-1058,1087-1099`; `.codex/agents/gsd-verifier.md:497-507`.

## Artifact Contract Pressure Points

- [d:c+r:i] `CONTEXT.md` is richer than the later traceability contract. The steering brief has explicit `D-XX` decisions, open questions, guardrails, and future-awareness buckets, but downstream enforcement only partially compiles that richness into machine-checkable obligations. `plan-phase` cross-checks plan objectives against context-described features, yet there is no required decision-by-decision mapping from each material `D-XX` into plan tasks and then into verification truths. Sources: `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/discuss-phase.md:971-1108`; `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/plan-phase.md:981-1004`; `.codex/agents/gsd-plan-checker.md:334-375`.
- [e:c+r:i] `canonical_refs` is a genuine strength. It is mandatory, path-based, propagated into downstream `<files_to_read>` blocks, and treated as source-of-truth input rather than ornamental documentation. This is one of the cleanest artifact contracts in the chain. Sources: `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/discuss-phase.md:468-476,867-873,1031-1051`; `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/plan-phase.md:291-304,370-375,828-833`; `.codex/get-shit-done/templates/context.md:93-110`.
- [d:c+r:i] `future_awareness -> future_preservation` is conceptually strong but only partially normalized. The planner is told to map each material future-aware item into one of four buckets, yet the artifact contract does not require per-item identifiers or a direct reconciliation table. That makes later audits possible, but more prose-dependent than they need to be. Sources: `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/discuss-phase.md:478-486,1076-1093`; `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/plan-phase.md:698-703,710-715`; `.codex/get-shit-done/templates/phase-prompt.md:25-34,143-149`.
- [d:c+r:i] `RESEARCH.md` is important but structurally under-specified relative to what planning asks it to do. Planning depends on research to validate open questions, preserve seams, and support validation architecture, yet there is no parallel artifact contract requiring explicit disposition of each open question or each working assumption before planning proceeds. Sources: `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/research-phase.md:34-57`; `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/plan-phase.md:383-391,420-444`.
- [d:c+r:i] `SUMMARY.md` is richer than the completion gate that consumes it. The summary template carries dependency graph, key files, decisions, deviations, and completed requirements, but `execute-phase` completion detection uses only a narrow subset of that richness. This is a classic place where the artifact contract is better than the live pressure applied to it. Sources: `.codex/get-shit-done/templates/summary.md:10-47,65-125,132-148`; `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/execute-phase.md:446-460,567-595`.
- [e:c+r:i] `DISCUSS-CHECKPOINT.json`, `DISCUSSION-LOG.md`, `HUMAN-UAT.md`, and `UAT.md` show good artifact-class hygiene. Partial state, canonical steering, audit trail, and human-testing state are kept distinct rather than silently promoted into one another. That aligns tightly with repo artifact-governance doctrine. Sources: `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/discuss-phase.md:891-927,941-943`; `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/execute-phase.md:1000-1058`; `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/verify-work.md:171-240`; `ARTIFACT-GOVERNANCE.md:7-41,66-85`.

## Verification / Review / Completion Pressure

- [e:c+r:i] The strongest excellence pressure in the chain currently lives in `gsd-plan-checker` and `gsd-verifier`, not in the more generic workflow routing. Scope-reduction blocking, requirement-orphan detection, anti-summary verification, and explicit `human_needed` handling are the most scrutiny-resistant parts of this surface. Sources: `.codex/agents/gsd-plan-checker.md:100-126,334-390,624-688`; `.codex/agents/gsd-verifier.md:18-30,142-152,375-391,495-540`.
- [e:c+r:i] The chain also meaningfully extends beyond artifact theater at the tail. Regression and schema-drift gates are real quality-pressure additions because they test prior behavior and false-positive build/type success rather than trusting that the produced artifacts tell the whole story. Sources: `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/execute-phase.md:827-949`; `WORKFLOW.md:96-109`.
- [d:c+r:i] The weakest review/completion pressure is not absence of checks but permissive disposition. Review is advisory, stalled planning can proceed anyway, requirements coverage gaps can be accepted anyway, auto-mode can auto-approve checkpoints, and human verification can advance after approval while leaving partial debt artifacts behind. The chain therefore offers excellence, but it does not consistently require excellence before status advancement. Sources: `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/plan-phase.md:902-918,993-1010`; `.codex/get-shit-done/references/checkpoints.md:4-12`; `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/execute-phase.md:744-775,1044-1058`.
- [d:c+r:i] The completion contract is deliberately asymmetric: execution completion is pragmatic and artifact-based, while final verification is goal-backward and stricter. That asymmetry is operationally sensible, but it also means the chain's early completion signals are more "proof of motion" than "proof of achievement." Sources: `.codex/get-shit-done/references/agent-contracts.md:13-18,56-79`; `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/execute-phase.md:446-460,954-999`.

## Strongest Justified Criticisms

- [d:c+r:i] Strongest criticism: the chain is excellence-capable but not excellence-demanding. Its upstream contracts preserve nuance, guardrails, and future seams unusually well, but its downstream closure posture still contains too many cheap exits: auto-approved checkpoints, advisory review, `Proceed anyway` branches, and approval-based advancement with partial human-verification debt. That combination structurally biases the system toward `green enough` when the repo's governing doctrine explicitly rejects that posture. Sources: `AGENTS.md:47-69`; `.planning/AGENTS.md:44-58,98-107`; `.codex/get-shit-done/references/checkpoints.md:4-12`; `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/plan-phase.md:902-918,993-1010`; `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/execute-phase.md:744-775,1044-1058`.
- [d:c+r:i] The chain front-loads epistemic richness and then only partially carries that richness forward. `CONTEXT.md` captures decisions, assumptions, open questions, canonical refs, and future-awareness explicitly, but the later contracts do not require equally explicit disposition mapping for every material steering item. That leaves later quality review more dependent on checker judgment and prose interpretation than the upstream artifact strength would justify. Sources: `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/discuss-phase.md:971-1108`; `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/plan-phase.md:698-703,981-1004`; `.codex/agents/gsd-plan-checker.md:334-375`.
- [d:c+r:i] Research is the least pressure-tested excellence surface in the chain. The repo says the rerun should avoid rediscovering doctrine sloppily and should preserve future-aware discipline, yet the research lane has no dedicated adequacy/disposition gate analogous to the plan checker or verifier. Sources: `.planning/readiness/phase-01-rerun/PLAN.md:132-145,560-563`; `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/research-phase.md:34-57`; `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/plan-phase.md:361-414`.

## Strategic Opportunities

- [p:r:i] Preserve the current strong upstream contract, but add stronger translation pressure rather than replacing judgment with more automation. The highest-value opportunity is better traceability from `CONTEXT.md` decisions/open questions/future-awareness into `PLAN.md` tasks and then into `VERIFICATION.md` or `UAT.md`. Sources: `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/discuss-phase.md:971-1108`; `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/plan-phase.md:694-703,981-1004`; `.planning/AGENTS.md:84-112`.
- [p:r:i] Introduce a bounded research adequacy/disposition check for non-trivial exploratory contexts. The chain does not need another generic gate everywhere, but it likely needs a way to force explicit treatment of the steering brief's open questions and provisional assumptions before they are allowed to disappear into planning prose. Sources: `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/discuss-phase.md:991-1029`; `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/research-phase.md:34-57`; `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/plan-phase.md:383-391`.
- [p:r:i] Distinguish `clean completion` from `debt-carrying completion` more explicitly. Right now review debt, pending human verification, and accepted coverage gaps can survive inside a nominally complete phase. The repo's quality bar would be better served if those states were harder to misread as equivalent. Sources: `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/plan-phase.md:993-1010`; `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/execute-phase.md:744-775,1000-1058,1087-1099`; `WORKFLOW.md:90-99`.
- [p:r:i] Treat auto-chain as a throughput lane, not as the presumptive excellence lane for doctrine-sensitive reruns. The current protocol makes sense for low-friction execution, but Checkpoint 4 is explicitly judging whether the workflow drives the best work the repo can currently support. On that standard, auto-approved checkpoints are a conscious tradeoff, not a neutral default. Sources: `.codex/get-shit-done/references/checkpoints.md:4-12`; `.planning/readiness/phase-01-rerun/AUDITS/checkpoint-4-workflow-harness-excellence-launch-spec.md:23-38`; `.planning/readiness/phase-01-rerun/PLAN.md:153-160,184-190`.
- [p:r:i] Keep machinery follow-through conditional. This lane shows several workflow-protocol weaknesses, but it does not by itself prove that more harness machinery is the right answer. Some fixes are likely protocol/template/checker changes rather than runtime surgery. Sources: `.planning/readiness/phase-01-rerun/PLAN.md:409-435,579-585`; `.planning/readiness/phase-01-rerun/AUDITS/checkpoint-4-workflow-harness-excellence-launch-spec.md:14-21,112-119`.
