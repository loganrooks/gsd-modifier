# Checkpoint 5 R5.18a2 Later-Lane And Quiet-Drop Adjudication Spec

Purpose: decide which broader 2026-04-15 governance concerns that current `R5.18` does not own now must be named as explicit later lanes, rather than being silently dropped or misclassified as already closed.

This is not a patch-authorization lane. It is a remainder-ownership lane.

## Scope

Adjudicate whether the following broader concerns:

- `live config/default posture alignment`
- `lifecycle-wide carry-forward outside progress/transition`
- `cheap helper/mechanical disposition ergonomics`
- `canonical local verify` plus narrow `repo-integrity` CI
- `remote review-owner routing`, issue/PR/MR templates, and linked review artifacts
- `worker-first exploration / one-active-substantive-task machinery`

should be:

- named later lanes
- folded into an already-named conditional lane
- explicitly accepted bounded risk
- or, if the evidence truly supports it, defended as out of current concern

## Required Output

Produce an `R5.18a2` adjudication artifact that:

- classifies each broader concern as `named-later-lane`, `fold-into-existing-lane`, `accepted bounded risk`, or `defended not-now`
- states why the concern is not a quiet drop
- names the owner and reopen trigger
- names the concrete later-lane id when applicable
- states whether any current-wave `R5.18a1` keep-out must be revised in light of this adjudication

## Anti-Misread Rules

- do not convert broader governance remainder into automatic `R5.18b/c` work by pressure alone
- do not let a real concern remain unnamed
- do not count stronger doctrine, prompt alignment, or package-local rigor as proof that a distinct control surface is closed
- do not flatten `later lane` into `ignored until later`
