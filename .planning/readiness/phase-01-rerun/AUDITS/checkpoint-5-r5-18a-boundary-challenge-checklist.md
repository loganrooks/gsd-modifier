## Global Challenge Rules

Every non-first-wave `R5.18a` decision must answer all of these:

1. What historical concern family does this file or decision touch?
2. Is the current move `promote-now`, `park-with-trigger`, `accepted bounded risk`, or `named later lane`?
3. If it stays out of first-wave, what is the explicit owner and reopen trigger?
4. If it stays out entirely, what named later lane owns it?
5. What evidence shows this is not a quiet drop?
6. What quality gain is being left on the table, and why is that acceptable now?
