## Why The GSD Split Was Load-Bearing

- [e:c:i] The readiness package explicitly treated the initial GSD split trigger as a Checkpoint 3 obligation, not as debt to push into Checkpoint 4. Both the launch spec and the readiness plan say that once the split fired, Checkpoint 3 owed the deeper GSD sublanes, a GSD-only synthesis, and only then the overall workflow/harness scope synthesis. Sources: `.planning/readiness/phase-01-rerun/AUDITS/checkpoint-3-workflow-harness-scope-launch-spec.md:45-50,72-89,105-114`; `.planning/readiness/phase-01-rerun/PLAN.md:314,345-353`; `.planning/readiness/phase-01-rerun/STATUS.md:15-18,39-40,72-74`; `.planning/readiness/phase-01-rerun/TASKS.md:11-13`.
- [e:c+r:i] The initial surface map could honestly identify GSD layers, but it could not honestly fix a later excellence-audit envelope as either `one GSD audit` or `a skill-family audit`. The deeper sublanes then showed why: workflow/artifact failures, role/doctrine drift, and runtime/config/overlay drift produce different kinds of evidence, different kinds of risk, and different follow-through ownership. Sources: `.planning/readiness/phase-01-rerun/AUDITS/checkpoint-3-gsd-surface-map.md:34-39,85-95,97-108`; `.planning/readiness/phase-01-rerun/AUDITS/checkpoint-3-gsd-workflow-chain-and-artifact-contracts.md:90-100`; `.planning/readiness/phase-01-rerun/AUDITS/checkpoint-3-gsd-agent-doctrine-and-role-contracts.md:64-76`; `.planning/readiness/phase-01-rerun/AUDITS/checkpoint-3-gsd-runtime-config-overlay-truth.md:83-102`.
- [d:r:i] Without the split, the later audit would have had to choose between false completeness and unbounded sprawl. A monolithic GSD lane would blur three distinct control surfaces together; a skill-count lane would inspect what is easiest to enumerate rather than what most needs scrutiny. Sources: `.planning/readiness/phase-01-rerun/AUDITS/checkpoint-3-workflow-harness-scope-launch-spec.md:54-57,61-68,84-89`; `.planning/readiness/phase-01-rerun/AUDITS/checkpoint-3-gsd-scope-synthesis-spec.md:75-79`; `.planning/readiness/phase-01-rerun/PLAN.md:357-360,378-386`.
