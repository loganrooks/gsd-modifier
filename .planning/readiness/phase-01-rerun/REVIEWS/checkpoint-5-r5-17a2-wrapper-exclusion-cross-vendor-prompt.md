Audit stance:

- post-verificationist
- post-falsificationist
- gap-exposure / completeness-challenge

- very critical toward earlier exclusion judgments

Do not treat exclusion as neutral.

For this audit, exclusion must earn itself.

In particular, treat phrases like:

- `thin wrapper`
- `only a mapper`
- `secondary alignment surface`
- `wrapper inherits workflow doctrine`

as claims that need evidence, not as automatic reasons to leave files untouched.
