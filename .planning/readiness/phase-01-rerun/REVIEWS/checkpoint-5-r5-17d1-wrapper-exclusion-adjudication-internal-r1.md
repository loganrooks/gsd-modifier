## Production-Condition Qualification

- [d:c+r:i] The production-condition qualification materially limits how the cross-vendor artifact is used, but it does not erase it. The compact prompt explicitly required the reviewer to read the on-disk bundle spec and lane spec (`.planning/readiness/phase-01-rerun/REVIEWS/checkpoint-5-r5-17a-wrapper-exclusion-cross-vendor-compact-prompt.md:5-14`), and the cross-vendor artifact itself records that compact-production condition up front (`.planning/readiness/phase-01-rerun/REVIEWS/checkpoint-5-r5-17a-wrapper-exclusion-cross-vendor-opus-r1.md:3-8`). That is enough to give its file-based judgments real standing on the original six-wrapper target set.
- [d:c+r:i] The qualification matters most where the cross-vendor artifact widened beyond that target set. The full prompt and the lane spec both enumerate exactly six wrapper targets and six delegated workflow surfaces (`.planning/readiness/phase-01-rerun/REVIEWS/checkpoint-5-r5-17a-wrapper-exclusion-cross-vendor-prompt.md:55-71`; `.planning/readiness/phase-01-rerun/REVIEWS/checkpoint-5-r5-17a-wrapper-exclusion-audit-spec.md:48-64`), but the cross-vendor output then promotes new wrapper claims for `gsd-autonomous`, `gsd-ship`, and `gsd-progress` (`.planning/readiness/phase-01-rerun/REVIEWS/checkpoint-5-r5-17a-wrapper-exclusion-cross-vendor-opus-r1.md:20-22,112-116,156-160`). Under the comparison policy, those widened claims are not weak or dismissible, but they remain `pressure` rather than sovereign adjudication because they are not mirrored by the internal lane and exceed the explicit six-wrapper target set. Sources: `.planning/readiness/phase-01-rerun/AUDIT-COMPARISON-POLICY.md:47-50,63-68,74-77,84-87`.
- [d:c+r:i] The resulting comparison rule is therefore asymmetric by design. Use the cross-vendor artifact at full weight for the original six-wrapper adjudication where it directly compared the same target set under the lane spec. Use it only as qualified challenge input for widened wrapper-family claims produced under compact-prompt conditions. That preserves the production truth instead of flattening the artifact into either `fully sovereign` or `discounted because compact`. Sources: `.planning/readiness/phase-01-rerun/REVIEWS/checkpoint-5-r5-17d1-wrapper-exclusion-adjudication-spec.md:29-37`; `.planning/readiness/phase-01-rerun/AUDIT-COMPARISON-POLICY.md:9-10,47-50,61-68,115-119`.
