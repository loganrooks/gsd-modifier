### 5. [o:c+r:i] `HIGH` — `gsd-research-phase/SKILL.md` defensibility (internal: edit target; cross-vendor: defensible)

Both lanes agree on the structural fact: the skill has no `<execution_context>` pointing to a workflow file, so its inlined spawn contract at `.codex/skills/gsd-research-phase/SKILL.md:74-224` is the operative authority. They disagree on what that implies.

This contradicts the live workflow's steering-brief / canonical-refs / `inconclusive items should not be treated as failure` posture at `.codex/get-shit-done/workflows/research-phase.md:39-49,52-80,90-96`. Presumptive edit target.
- Cross-vendor (`:92-94`): because there is no `<execution_context>` delegation, there is no transitive doctrine contradiction in the wrapper-inheritance sense; the wrapper IS the workflow; defensible exclusion.

Adjudication: internal's reading survives better under direct reread. Cross-vendor's structural test — `no delegation, therefore no contradiction` — is the weaker frame because it treats wrapper-as-workflow as making the wrapper safer, when the POST-FALSIFICATIONIST doctrine should read it as making the wrapper MORE load-bearing, not less. `AGENTS.md:89` specifically says `Preserve and express earned distinctions explicitly. Do not let repeated naming density, shorthand, or omission quietly choose winners between live branches`, which is exactly the hazard when a standalone skill silently overrides the live workflow's newer doctrine. Direct verification at `.codex/skills/gsd-research-phase/SKILL.md:162` confirms the prescriptive `Be prescriptive, not exploratory` language is present verbatim.

Recommended standing: lean to internal (`presumptive edit target`), but explicit disposition required in `R5.17d` final or `R5.18`.

### 6. [o:c+r:i] `MEDIUM-HIGH` — `gsd-execute-phase/SKILL.md` scope-framing (internal: defensible; cross-vendor: under-justified conditional)

Both are reasonable; the operational difference is small because the required follow-through is identical — the Track C trunk must land in `execute-phase.md` either way.

Recommended standing: accept cross-vendor framing (`mandatory conditional disposition`) as the better fit for the `R5.17/R5.18` boundary, with internal's warrant preserved as a fallback if Track C lands cleanly.
