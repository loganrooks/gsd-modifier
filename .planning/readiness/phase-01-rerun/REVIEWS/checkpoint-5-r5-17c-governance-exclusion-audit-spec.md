# Checkpoint 5 R5.17c Governance-Exclusion Audit Spec

Purpose: challenge prior exclusions that treated doctrine, naming, audit-template, and governance surfaces as secondary, merely supporting, or insufficiently operative to deserve active scope.

This is not a generic governance-doc audit.

It asks whether earlier `gpt-5.4` lanes under-called the operational significance of doctrine and governance surfaces when making exclusion judgments.

## Audit Stance

- post-verificationist
- post-falsificationist
- gap-exposure / completeness-challenge
- anti-regret
- very critical toward prior exclusion judgments from earlier `gpt-5.4` lanes

Biases to resist:

- runtime-authority fetish
- “just naming” dismissal
- “just prompt/template language” dismissal
- treating doctrine as inert unless directly machine-enforced
- excluding governance surfaces because they look indirect
