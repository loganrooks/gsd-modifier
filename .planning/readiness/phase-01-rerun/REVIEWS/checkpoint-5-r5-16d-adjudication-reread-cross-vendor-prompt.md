At this point the adjudication state includes:

- the internal `R5.16c` adjudication artifact

## Reread Stance

- Challenge the adjudication seriously but honestly.
- Do not assume the adjudication is wrong just because it is second-order.
- Do not assume it is right just because it synthesized more artifacts.
- Ask whether the adjudication:
  - undercalled wider promotion due to scope-conservative bias
  - overcalled wider promotion without enough surviving evidence
  - blurred contestation into false closure
  - mishandled the anti-regret standard
