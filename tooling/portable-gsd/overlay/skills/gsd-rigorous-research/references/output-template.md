# Output Template

Use this as a lightweight contract, not a rigid form. Keep the sections that add signal.

## Minimum Sections

```markdown
# [Title]

## Research Frame
- Mode:
- Question:
- Scope:
- Non-goals:
- Stop condition:

## Path Of Inquiry
- Entry point:
- Branches considered:
- Branches pursued:
- Branches deferred or abandoned:
- Unexpected branches / reframings:

## Assumptions Surfaced
- [assumption] - why it matters, current status, what could falsify it

## Evidence Base
### Direct evidence
- cited facts, local docs, code observations, measurements

### Inference and interpretation
- what follows from the evidence

### Unknowns
- what cannot yet be claimed honestly

## Dependencies And Relations
| Item | Depends on | Constrains or affects | Vulnerability |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ... | ... | ... | ... |

## Scope Expansions And Deferrals
- Defer / Follow-and-mark / Revisit later entries as needed

## Strengthening Opportunities
- [current strength] - bounded intensification move, optionality effect, proposed carry route

## What Has Strong Carry Now
- decisions or conclusions supported by the current evidence

## What Still Needs Wider Carry
- unresolved questions, weakly supported areas, future work

## Sources
- local paths
- external sources
```

## Mode-Specific Emphasis

### Terrain mapping
- Add `Option Space` after `Assumptions Surfaced`
- Do not force a recommendation

### Hypothesis testing
- Add `Hypothesis`
- Add `Disconfirming Evidence`
- End with `Hypothesis status: survives / weakened / rejected / underdetermined`

### Solution evaluation
- Add `Criteria`
- Add `Option Comparison`
- Recommendation is allowed only if the evidence justifies it

### Synthesis
- Add `Artifacts Read`
- Add `Integrated Decision Structure`
- Preserve tensions and deferrals instead of flattening them away

## Phase-Adjacent Handoff

If this research is feeding later GSD planning, add:

- `Planning Handoff`
  - what now carries strongly enough to treat as decided
  - what remains assumed or open
  - derived constraints
  - future-awareness seams to preserve
  - strengthening opportunities plus carry route (`current-phase intensification` or `seeded later resurfacing`)
  - deferred ideas or follow-up research lanes
